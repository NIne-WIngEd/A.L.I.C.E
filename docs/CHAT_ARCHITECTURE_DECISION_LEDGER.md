# July 27, 2026 Architecture Decision Ledger

This ledger records the decision sequence that produced the consolidated architecture.

1. **Personal knowledge architecture:** personal facts need not be baked into the base model; memory and retrieval provide inspectable knowledge.
2. **Predictive personalization:** A.L.I.C.E. must infer preferences and missing context through evidence-linked beliefs, user models, embeddings, and calibrated predictions.
3. **Continuous learning:** every interaction, search, tool result, correction, code outcome, and observation may become a learning event; promotion remains selective.
4. **Procedural and parametric learning:** A.L.I.C.E. may create reusable skills, write code, improve its implementation, train specialized components, and eventually update personal model weights.
5. **Independent judgment:** A.L.I.C.E. develops its own evidence-based voice and may disagree; Rayan retains final constitutional authority and operational override.
6. **Final cognitive architecture:** executive control, world/user/self/causal models, metacognition, hierarchical planning, specialist agents, multimodal perception, universal action, scientific discovery, formal verification, resource intelligence, social intelligence, and embodiment are permanent capability domains.
7. **Ambition-first governance:** inherited least-agency/default-deny rules do not govern the destination system. Only the minimal authority kernel is specially protected.
8. **Project-wide evolvability:** documents, code, schemas, tests, validators, and workflows from Phases 1–4 may be changed when the destination architecture requires it.
9. **Consumer product:** a host-neutral Personal Cognitive Kernel supports A.L.I.C.E. and the consumer distribution internally codenamed Friday.
10. **Separation schedule:** kernel extraction begins at Phase 5.0; formal repository/product separation occurs at Phase 6.5.
11. **Host-selected identity:** Friday is not the user's assistant name. Each host selects a name, voice, and identity locally.
12. **Full parity:** the consumer system inherits the full generalizable destination capability set of A.L.I.C.E.; A.L.I.C.E. may lead experiments but cannot become a permanently separate capability class.
13. **Long-term handoff:** after a dedicated consumer maintenance team exists, Rayan may return primary focus to A.L.I.C.E. while parity continues through the shared-kernel process.
