# Consumer Product Handoff and Maintenance Plan

**Status:** Long-term operating plan

## 1. Before dedicated team formation

Rayan and the core project maintain:

- A.L.I.C.E.;
- the shared Personal Cognitive Kernel;
- the consumer distribution internally codenamed Friday;
- common evaluation and migration systems.

A.L.I.C.E. remains the frontier research deployment. Consumer product requirements are considered during shared-kernel design so successful capabilities can be generalized rather than rewritten.

## 2. Team-formation gate

A dedicated consumer maintenance team should exist before broad public scale. Minimum ownership areas:

- desktop and operating-environment releases;
- model and hardware compatibility;
- local storage and migration;
- security response and signed updates;
- user support and diagnostics;
- product UX and onboarding;
- capability productization;
- release evaluation and rollback;
- documentation and developer ecosystem.

## 3. After handoff

After the team demonstrates independent release and incident capability:

- Rayan may return primary attention to A.L.I.C.E.;
- A.L.I.C.E. continues as the personal flagship and frontier research system;
- the kernel remains a shared upstream dependency;
- the consumer team owns downstream release cadence;
- successful A.L.I.C.E. capabilities enter the parity ledger;
- consumer-specific fixes flow upstream when generally useful.

## 4. No abandonment by architecture

The handoff does not freeze the consumer product. The team inherits the commitment to all ratified A.L.I.C.E. capability classes and the frontier-research register.

If the consumer company later chooses a narrower commercial edition, that edition is a packaging decision. The complete host-owned architecture remains an available product track rather than being deleted from the roadmap.
