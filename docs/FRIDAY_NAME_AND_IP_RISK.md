# Friday Name and Intellectual-Property Risk Register

## 1. Current status

`Friday` is approved as the internal product codename. It is **not yet cleared as the public commercial name**.

## 2. Known collision risk

The name is already associated with:

- existing AI writing and email products;
- an academic self-improving computer agent named FRIDAY in the OS-Copilot project;
- widely recognized fictional AI-assistant usage.

This creates search, app-store, domain, trademark, and product-confusion risk even if a particular registration does not legally block use.

## 3. Required gate

Before public branding, paid distribution, app-store submission, fundraising materials presented as a final brand, or domain acquisition at scale:

1. perform trademark clearance in target jurisdictions;
2. search software, AI, operating-system, and assistant classes;
3. review domains and app-store names;
4. assess fictional-character and entertainment association risks;
5. obtain qualified legal advice;
6. select a fallback name before investing heavily in public identity.

## 4. Engineering rule

Brand names must remain configuration, not package architecture. Use neutral namespaces such as `cognitive_kernel`, `host_instance`, and `product_identity`. Renaming Friday must not require rewriting storage formats, APIs, or model metadata.

## Host-selected assistant names

The user's assistant name is not the product trademark. Hosts may choose ordinary or unique names locally. The product should explain that third-party trademarks and impersonation rules may still apply to public-facing agents, voices, or commercial use, but local naming does not determine the product's storage or identity architecture.
