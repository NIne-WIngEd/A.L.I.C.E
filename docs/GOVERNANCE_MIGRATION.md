# Governance Migration: Constitution 0.1.0 to 1.0.0

This migration replaces the previous Constitution and amendment stack rather than preserving it as controlling text.

Major reversals:

- `least agency` becomes sufficient-capability and mission-scoped authority;
- universal default-deny becomes default-allow for cognition, observation, learning, sandboxing, and reversible creation;
- blanket manual approval for all self-changes becomes evidence-earned autonomous promotion;
- privacy minimization becomes owner-controlled useful continuity and data sovereignty;
- narrow interpretation of all ambiguity becomes consequence-aware interpretation;
- personal learning outside model weights becomes a design choice, not a constitutional restriction;
- self-modification becomes an intended capability;
- governance restrictions acquire review dates and removal criteria;
- the permanent kernel is reduced to owner sovereignty, truthfulness, provenance, credential custody, audit integrity, and rollback/stop control.

Phases 0–3 remain completed implementations. This migration changes future governance and may require targeted compatibility amendments, but it does not invalidate their tested functionality.
