# Host-Selected Identity Standard

**Status:** Ratified product-family standard
**Applies to:** Consumer product codename Friday and any future general distribution

## 1. Three distinct names

The architecture separates:

1. `product_codename` — internal development label, currently `friday`;
2. `product_brand` — future commercial application or platform name;
3. `assistant_name` — the local identity chosen by the host.

No schema, encryption domain, storage path, API, or model checkpoint may depend on the public brand or the host's display name.

## 2. Host naming

During enrollment, the host may choose:

- assistant name;
- pronunciation;
- voice;
- form of address for the host;
- initial personality and judgment settings;
- whether the assistant may propose later identity evolution.

A temporary autogenerated name may be used before enrollment, but the product must not force the internal codename as the personal identity.

## 3. Identity evolution

The assistant may develop a more distinct voice and self-model through experience. It may propose changes to its name, voice, or identity presentation, but the host retains final authority over displayed identity.

Identity evolution must not silently modify:

- the product identifier;
- host ownership;
- encryption keys;
- audit identity;
- constitutional authority.

## 4. Identity Capsule

The Identity Capsule includes:

- stable host instance identifier;
- host-selected assistant name and history;
- voice and interaction configuration;
- constitutional relationship;
- memories, beliefs, skills, and learned parameters;
- model and kernel compatibility metadata.

Moving the Identity Capsule to another base model or machine preserves the personal assistant's identity.

## 5. Privacy

The assistant name and identity state are personal host data. They must not be required in vendor telemetry, licensing identifiers, crash reports, or public model registries.
