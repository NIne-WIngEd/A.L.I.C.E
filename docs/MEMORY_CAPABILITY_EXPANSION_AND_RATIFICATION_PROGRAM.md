## 1. Governing decision

A.L.I.C.E. pursues maximum useful capability under owner sovereignty, truthful state, inspectable authority, reversible evolution, privacy, provenance, product isolation, deletion, and evidence-backed engineering.

Capability gates are activation criteria. They are not general research bans or permanent architecture ceilings.

## 2. Scope

This program governs memory, evidence, claims, graphs, vectors, objects, workflows, context, models, datasets, training, synchronization, deployment, migration, deletion, rollback, and public claims.

It replaces the broad Memory Architecture Hold with profile-specific authority.

## 5. Enabling invariants

### Owner sovereignty

The owner controls constitutional authority, production activation, correction, export, deletion, rollback, pause, revocation, and shutdown.

### Truthfulness and epistemic integrity

Evidence, claims, inferences, predictions, generated reconstructions, operational state, and implementation status remain distinct.

### Privacy and product isolation

Private payloads, owner-specific state, source-person material, credentials, keys, and private model artifacts remain outside public Git and outside Friday or another host unless explicitly exported.

### Provenance

Material memory, projection, dataset, model, action, and decision influence is traceable.

## Product and host isolation

A.L.I.C.E., Friday, the Personal Cognitive Kernel, and every owner or host namespace remain isolated by default. Cross-product or cross-host transfer requires an explicit owner-authorized export with provenance, minimization, deletion, and rollback semantics.

## Deletion

Deletion is coordinated across authoritative and derived systems, restores, datasets, replay, and model influence. Technical limits remain visible.

### Rollback

Material changes preserve a known-good recovery path or record irreversibility and containment.

These invariants enable ambitious autonomy and experimentation.

## 8. Review and removal

Every temporary activation gate has a review date or event, measurable removal criterion, and successor path.

A gate that cannot explain how evidence could remove or evolve it is invalid unless it is an enabling invariant or an exact owner constitutional decision.
