# MFM research decision: formation, reconstruction and authority

**Date:** 2026-09-29. **Scope:** A.L.I.C.E. MFM workstream. This is a research and implementation decision, not a trained-model or Stage G qualification claim. Compared `research/frontier-watch@8cf5a6f8`, N0 branch `@a19f8e88`, FBM `@50272386`, and MFM `@b6741db3` before this increment. The scan covered agent memory formation, retrieval, temporal revision, forgetting, multimodal analysis and selected competitors. Primary papers and authors' system descriptions were favored over rankings or product claims.

## N0 and FBM transfer

N0's full-envelope branch reached source-bound CPU and public-mixture gates, then exposed CUDA fp16 failures only on a production-path P43 run. Its latest recorded lesson is that partial unit/autocast checks are diagnostic, not a full joint-system receipt. A previous transfer manifest also admitted sealed FINAL paths despite a reassuring boolean. For MFM this means exact source/weights/data/split receipts, independent entry audits, full-path evaluation, immutable failed attempts, and no post-hoc hotfix escalation. A schema test cannot become a learning claim. Magnolia or Kaggle placement becomes useful only once rights-cleared training data, independently checked targets and a full objective are assembled. GPU availability alone cannot make three fictional training rows adequate.

FBM's `TRACE_SCHEMA_v0.1.md` and `FBM_DATA_AND_SEED_PROGRAM_2026-09-26.md` require a compact procedure trace for material work, then a **separate** eligible input/target/authority/outcome case. The public MFM contract fixture is diagnostic. It has no independent sealed gold and no observed consumer outcome. Its traces remain `procedure_only`, `partial` and private-payload-free.

## Current decision and staged path

1. **Now:** close transitive source ancestry on MFM input. A selected derivative must bring registered, authorized, same-host, as-of eligible and hash-verified parents into the packet. Cycles, missing ancestry and revocation fail before bytes are returned. A read receipt records the closure separately from retrieval routes. This is a concrete prerequisite for later task-conditioned reconstruction, correction and deletion. It does not decide truth.
2. **Next:** build rights-cleared longitudinal formation training material with independent adjudication and split by host, source family, generator family and time. Train a host-neutral learned MFM on evidence pointers, subject, valid/known time, competing interpretations and abstention. Keep Rayan/Elaina facts out of shared weights. Attach exact artifact and data lineage. Use Magnolia/Kaggle for the **full admitted training objective**, not a tiny demo.
3. **Then:** compare formation against capable extraction and long-context baselines on identical opened evidence. Integrate the independent authority gate and selected KurrentDB/XTDB/Qdrant/graph/object planes. Measure accepted projections, future source reconstruction, retrieval, native personal judgment, corrections and delayed deletion influence. Failure in subject, provenance or deletion blocks promotion regardless of average score.

The physical stack and full consumer capability destination remain those on current main. A.L.I.C.E.'s memory model interprets experience; the deterministic source and Claim gates own authority. This branch has no real selected-stack read, trained weights or full-loop pass yet. Do not substitute prompt heuristics, invented biography, fixed store counts or mock model scores for those missing observations.
