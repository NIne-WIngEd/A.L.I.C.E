## Destination and boundaries

Fable's reusable, learned MFM interprets an authorized experience in context
and emits source-citing, time-scoped, uncertain `MemoryProposalBundle` entries.
V1 uses the separately verified, locally cloned, pretrained non-IT Gemma source
as a licensed representation ancestor and initializes its own formation
decoder. The future first-party-base route replaces that ancestor with a
public-data semantic/sensory foundation trained from fresh weights. These
specialized weights encode **formation
competence**, not a particular user's biography. For A.L.I.C.E., Rayan's corpus supplies host evidence and selected
formation tasks; Elaina's evidence is a separate source-person axis; A.L.I.C.E.'s
post-activation history belongs to her own self. Consumer Fable has a host and
developing self without requiring a separate source person. FBM must assemble
and evaluate these roles from authorized inputs without depending on a paid
teacher or asking every consumer for manually labeled gold. During research,
assistant teaching may provide attributed public examples and contrasts as in
N0. The future foundation does not inherit outside weights; V1 explicitly
does, with its different origin preserved in lineage and product claims.

The formation output is a **proposal**. Registered Experience and source bytes
remain authoritative evidence; a deterministic gate authenticates and decides
Claim writes, correction, revocation and deletion. Accepted projections feed
host, relationship, assistant-self, episode, mission, graph, vector and
procedural systems. Retrieval and native judgment must show an actual later
effect. MFM cannot choose the physical store, infer consent from content, or
turn a plausible reconstruction into the person's history.

The 64-page *The Second Mind* comic supplies destination cases, not evidence
that they already work. Pages 18–21 distinguish source, inference, unknown,
fictional rehearsal and host identity. Pages 33–39 require mixed outcomes,
decision rationale, recurring patterns, negotiated norms and tested skills.
Pages 40 and 49–56 require continuity across engine/device change, derivative
deletion, calibrated uncertainty and changing preferences. A.L.I.C.E.'s
ratified memory/identity architecture makes those subject and authority
boundaries explicit. The current `main` Phase-2-to-Fable-v1 execution plan
defines MFM-1 through MFM-6; its Stage G matrix is selected-stack-first.

This is an order of dependency and validation, **not** a model-size, context,
history, modality, device or capability ceiling. CPU contract tests can prove a
boundary; they do not satisfy a trained-model or Stage G gate. The selected
physical defaults are KurrentDB for Experience, XTDB v2 for bitemporal Claim,
NATS JetStream for device ingress, Qdrant for vector/multimodal retrieval,
LadybugDB locally or NebulaGraph for scale-out graph, and encrypted
content-addressed object custody. MFM's packet stays backend-neutral. We use
these selected planes through owned interfaces; a challenger requires a concrete
capability or operational failure, not a new backend tournament.

## Current implementation and next increment

`formation_context_planner.py`, `formation_sources.py` and
`formation_retrieval.py` now establish the A.L.I.C.E.-owned Lane 1 interface:
arbitrary retrieval planes return source IDs; a registered private source
service supplies actor/subject/time/lineage, custody and permission; exact
digest-verified bytes enter the packet; read receipts bind the opened IDs. The
all-registered selector is an explicit reference baseline, **not** a learned
planner or runtime integration. `formation_gold.py` and `formation_candidate.py`
now load and score a frozen, public, multi-host contract fixture. The exact
learning admission boundary and remaining evidence are in
[`MFM_LEARNING_ADMISSION_2026-09-29.md`](MFM_LEARNING_ADMISSION_2026-09-29.md).
Keep private corpus manifests and raw payloads outside the public branch.
FBM process traces remain procedure seeds until an eligible case binds actual
input, target, authority result and outcome.
The former *instruction-tuned backbone* commands in
[`MFM_GPU_EXECUTION_HANDOFF_2026-09-29.md`](MFM_GPU_EXECUTION_HANDOFF_2026-09-29.md)
are retired. V1 instead stages the verified non-IT publisher source, creates
a separate MFM role clone and trains its fresh formation decoder; see
[`MFM_V1_SPECIALIST_TRAINING_PATH_2026-09-30.md`](MFM_V1_SPECIALIST_TRAINING_PATH_2026-09-30.md).
Public pack inventory and fresh foundation design remain future-base work.
The current data and code cannot support a native pretraining cost or
capability estimate yet.

## Lessons applied from N0 and FBM

- Preserve exact source, split, model, evaluator and environment lineage. A
  preflight, partial forward, static test and full gradient/evaluation have
  different authority; report them separately.
- If a critical capability fails, localize source attribution, representation,
  context, model, gate and downstream use before changing architecture. Avoid
  an accumulation of hotfixes to hide a wrong interface or absent learning.
- Keep synthetic rehearsal visibly synthetic, private host data host-scoped,
  and a generated interpretation provisional. Correct the source and derivative
  graph rather than promoting plausible text to historical truth.
- Record one compact FBM trace for a material construction operation; make
  supervised cases only when independent labels and observed outcomes exist.
