# Full-role sequence successor: material and caller-input construction

Status: authorized development after verified Magnolia 576984; not a new
trained model, an admitted FINAL corpus, or product qualification.

The result and original failures are preserved in
[the mechanism record](MFM_SEQUENCE_FAILURE_DISCRIMINATOR_2026-10-07.md).
Formation must still propose supported meaning, subject, time, uncertainty,
sensitivity and prior-state changes across episodes, relationships, norms,
skills, missions and outcomes. Claim alone owns writes. Native modalities and
accepted-memory consumer feedback remain required subsequent construction.

## Decision and research boundary

The owner reaffirmed the foundation-before-personalization boundary on October 7.
Gemma supplies the frozen base source representations in this selected route;
MFM builds its own learned foundation, formation objectives and governed consumer
contract on top. No Gemma answer head or pretrained language bridge is adopted.
The public conditional stage is a foundation prerequisite; it does not substitute
for full formation learning or owner-independent Alice/Fable qualification.

The measured failure is incomplete source-conditioned authored sequence learning:
rare and unseen references, semantic text and composition remain wrong under
correct prefixes. Cache/EOS-only repair, unchanged extra epochs, arbitrary syntax
repair and a reference-only result cannot address that failure class.

There are two real missing dependencies shared by the plausible successors:
complete independently supplied input inventories, and diverse conditional
sequence teaching material. Build those now. This is an explicit construction
stage inside the full-role route, not a qualification program that displaces it.

The next model decision compares fresh conditional pretraining, licensed lexical
transfer, and a larger pretrained-language/native-formation assembly. The
governing licensed-base direction permits traced derived tensors with separately
initialized formation-specific weights. The current selected source-only path
explicitly forbids using the pretrained lexical/language head. Any departure
needs a new versioned initialization and consumer interface; it cannot be a
fallback or a relabeled continuation of the old fit.

The [route comparison](research/MFM_SEQUENCE_ROUTE_COMPARISON_2026-10-07.md),
[independent design review](research/MFM_NEXT_ROUTE_DESIGN_REVIEW_2026-10-07.md)
and [material review](research/MFM_CONDITIONAL_TEACHING_MATERIALS_2026-10-07.md)
record primary mechanisms, conflicting evidence, compute requirements and
transfer limits. In particular, pretrained embeddings alone do not import an
autoregressive language model. Width reduction and frozen lexical bridges are
not established fixes. No such transformation is adopted in this stage.

## Separate caller input

The current admission packages context within teacher records. Its source-view
proves that old identity strings appear in inputs, but does not establish a
complete typed registry. The input-only audit finds 925 opened evidence
occurrences and only five explicit prior claims. The existing catalog cannot
encode all 441 decisions: scopes are unregistered, with additional subject and
prior-state gaps. Preserve this audit and the original data unchanged.

Introduce a separately versioned caller-input manifest, supplied before teacher
admission. It contains the existing exact v1.6 context, every opened source byte
in context order, and an optional explicit read-only prior-registry snapshot.
Validate context, original-byte digests, namespace, host scope and snapshot
version before exposing selection candidates. No teacher decision, expected
action, regex-mined identifier or label-derived inventory enters this API.

Candidate eligibility follows the actual consumer responsibilities:

- Opened evidence can be cited and anchored; metadata-only prior or withheld
  evidence can be a governance target but cannot become a citation.
- Registered entity/scene/mission/workspace targets retain their supporting
  sources and registration provenance.
- A source's declared subject can identify a proposal subject without becoming
  a newly registered entity target or episode participant.
- Prior claim/episode/uncertainty/evidence records come only from the explicit
  scoped read snapshot, retaining generation, status and record digests.
- Disposition scopes remain extensible semantic labels. Their matching within
  the proposal/disposition output is checked by the original contract; they
  must not be converted into a closed caller identity vocabulary.

The new manifest grants no truth, read permission, write permission or live
registry currency. Production authorities must re-read policy and registration.
Original validators stay unchanged. Caller digests and identity strings are
bookkeeping; a future learned selector must learn candidate choice, including
distractors, order changes, renaming and absent alternatives.

## Conditional teaching material

Prepare the original E2E training release at its exact Git revision, with its
original CC BY-SA 4.0 grant, retained legal notice and attribution. It supplies
human meaning-representation-to-text examples, not formation labels or real
owner memory. Preserve original inputs and reference strings. Group alternative
references by canonical semantic input before deterministic training/development
assignment and exact-pair deduplication. Official development/test data never
enter training. Neither these language-development rows nor the old nine
formation development cases are independent MFM FINAL.

This initial material broadens conditional text teaching; its restaurant domain
does not establish full language competence or sufficient scale. Dolly passage
examples remain held pending item-level provenance. Conflicting/noncommercial
WikiAuto, DialogSum and mixed-origin DART grants cannot be treated as a blanket
commercial-training admission.

Run a complete token census with the exact prepared Gemma processor, no model
weights or optimizer. Preserve complete inputs and targets, BOS/EOS handling,
token frequencies and largest source/target cases. No truncation or vocabulary
reduction is allowed. The census determines the actual source-feature preparation
and training allocation. Public-only material may use a free GPU route without
claiming that route can process private owner data. The protected private CPU
route and its isolation requirements remain intact.

## Construction continuation

After the census, prepare conditional source features and train the researched
versioned sequence producer, then integrate learned identity/span selection and
expanded full-role formation teaching in that same producer. Do not select a
full-fit budget from row counts alone. Keep initialization/control lineage,
actual stress backward/restart, positive grounded free generation, semantic
fields and governed consumer effects observable. A foundation-material or
caller-manifest pass is only the prerequisite it actually proves.
