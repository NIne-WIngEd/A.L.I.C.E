## Research and construction implications

[Random-decoder transfer §3](https://aclanthology.org/2020.tacl-1.18.pdf) and
[sequential pretraining §2.1.5](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.598.pdf)
inherit lexical embeddings and use far more data. They do not establish that
441 narrow examples should teach our fresh lexical head.
[MemReader](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.07877v2) starts from pretrained Qwen
language competence. [Memory Decoder at Scale](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.27919)
uses pretrained backbones and base-answer interpolation, unlike governed MFM.
[BART](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.703.pdf) and
[MASS](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/song19d/song19d.pdf) support broader
conditional sequence teaching, not a predicted sufficient budget for this model.

[RAT-SQL typed pointers](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.677.pdf) support
learned reference selection from caller inventories. Existing catalog compilation
does not implement a learned selector. [Fairseq copying](https://github.com/facebookresearch/fairseq/blob/3d262bb25690e4eb2e7d3c1309b1e9c406ca4b99/examples/pointer_generator/pointer_generator_src/transformer_pg.py)
requires explicit source identity correspondence. Blind scattering of processed
input IDs would mix source content with instruction/context/template tokens and
does not guarantee original UTF-8/canonical escape alignment. Copying cannot
author paraphrases or decide memory relevance, uncertainty, time or evolution.

Next construction must use the diagnostic result and preserve one full MFM
producer. Complete caller inventories, exact source alignment, diverse rights-
admitted conditional teaching, full-role cases and accepted-memory consumer
evidence remain distinct work. No architecture or additional fit is selected
solely because this candidate failed; no arbitrary training extension follows.
