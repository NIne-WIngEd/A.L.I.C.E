## Research consulted before construction

Current frontier-watch is3aedd37d7612427905b46db06c91ef1622ee29c1: October5
explicit belief/gap recovery and October6 source independence, active intent and
capability evolution. Those mechanisms inform full-role evidence and authority;
they do not supply fresh-head language competence.
[BART](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.703.pdf) and
[MASS](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/song19d/song19d.pdf) support conditional
sequence teaching, but train their representations jointly on far more data.
Our complete frozen-source reconstruction transfers only that learning role.
[Multi-task sequence learning §4.3.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.06114) shows that
strong reconstruction can harm the primary task, which requires separate losses
and raw formation outcomes here; the mixture is a bounded construction candidate.

[Pointer Networks](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/5866-pointer-networks.pdf),
[RAT-SQL §4.3–4.4](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.677.pdf) and its
[typed pointer implementation](https://github.com/microsoft/rat-sql/blob/051e7d35f3092d2c75b64dc0c7f1d791942d4f19/ratsql/models/nl2code/decoder.py#L858-L881)
support selecting input candidates rather than generating opaque identity.
[Fairseq's Transformer pointer generator](https://github.com/facebookresearch/fairseq/blob/3d262bb25690e4eb2e7d3c1309b1e9c406ca4b99/examples/pointer_generator/pointer_generator_src/transformer_pg.py#L238-L362)
and [OpenNMT's copy alignment](https://github.com/OpenNMT/OpenNMT-py/blob/97111d97551c24857076a4102eabdb468b35cff4/onmt/modules/copy_generator.py#L60-L139)
require explicit source identity maps. Frozen hidden states alone do not provide
that contract. [Low-resource copying negative results](https://aclanthology.org/2024.insights-1.9.pdf)
and [scratch structured extraction](https://aclanthology.org/2020.spnlp-1.6.pdf)
rule out assuming a pointer or441examples suffices for our full role.
[MemReader](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.07877v2) starts from pretrained language
competence; [HaluMem](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.03506v3) separates extraction,
update and downstream usefulness. Neither supplies evidence for our fresh model.
