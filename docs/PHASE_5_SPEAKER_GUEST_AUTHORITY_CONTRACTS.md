# Phase 5 Speaker, Guest, and Authority Contracts

P5.0e extends the host-neutral Personal Cognitive Kernel with metadata-only speaker context, ephemeral guest sessions, visible purpose-bound guest grants, and explainable authority decisions.

## Guarantees

- Speaker context records confidence, evidence class, uncertainty, scope, and authority ceiling without raw audio or voice profiles.
- Voice matching alone never authorizes privileged or high-consequence actions.
- Guest grants are explicit, visible, scoped, expiring, revocable, non-delegable, and unable to expand themselves.
- Guest sessions hide private views, require local action logging, and carry no persistent authority or private state.
- Private data, messaging, purchases, system changes, destructive actions, credentials, and production governance require stronger authority.
- Owner-only capabilities remain bound to explicit owner approval.
- Every contract is deterministic, tamper-evident, product/host/encryption scoped, and metadata only.

## Deferred

P5.0e does not implement microphone capture, biometric recognition, speaker models, persistent guest storage, autonomous authority escalation, the Phase 6 interface, or Friday product source.
