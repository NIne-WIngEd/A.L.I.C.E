## 4. Two sequencing details that must not get lost

### Experiments follow their dependencies

The master handoff's compact arrow sequence mentions E01–E07 before Stage G. Read that as an experiment programme, not a requirement to execute every experiment at that point. The machine ledger explicitly gives E05 the dependency `Stage G complete; G+ protocol frozen`. Treating every experiment as pre-G would create a cycle.

| Experiment | What it must establish | Dependency and placement |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Learned identity improves judgment over matched prompting/retrieval. | Eligible identity training and independent evaluation; personality qualification in G. |
| E02 | Trained AMFM improves memory formation over a general-model proposal baseline. | Eligible AMFM data/training; model-plane qualification in G. |
| E03 | Meaning survives formation, storage, projections, retrieval, corrections and restart. | Required fabric implementation and deployment profile; integrated G evidence. |
| E04 | Experience improves continuing missions against reset/frozen behavior at matched budget. | Full Mission Graph and phase eligibility; continuing-mission qualification. |
| E05 | Structured recollection improves utility at measured latency/token/storage cost. | Accepted G and frozen G+ protocol; belongs in G+. |
| E06 | Replacing the executive preserves commitments, values, source attribution and behavior. | Compatible candidates and migration eligibility; continuity qualification and applicable later rechecks. |
| E07 | Corrections/deletions propagate through live state, restore, projections and training descendants. | Complete lineage/revocation implementation; G evidence and applicable migration rechecks. |

No completed E01–E07 result is recorded in the current experiment ledger. New numeric acceptance thresholds must be frozen before their measurements; this roadmap does not invent them or weaken existing hard gates.

### Stage G scope was amended, not silently closed

Frozen main still contains the older requirement to stress every registered candidate and compare every same-role pair. Rayan accepted a different planning scope: qualify one declared deployment profile and its complete stack, with credible challengers addressing identified uncertainty and justified interaction coverage. The complete research registry and replacement paths remain.

That accepted decision must be translated into the canonical machine policy and acceptance matrix before Stage G closure. This document does not select a backend or claim that translation is already complete. Full logical memory semantics, correction/deletion/recovery/scale requirements and source/host/product isolation remain required.

The separate accepted A-SYN breadth direction also needs its explicit future expansion/refreeze lineage. The current 287 pool remains unchanged. Reopening held family gaps or changing synthesis breadth is not a provider repair.

## 5. What Alice should be when we reach the destination

Alice should be one continuing entity with learned Elaina-derived identity, learned memory formation, understanding of Rayan, relationship state, self-history, skills, goals and a replaceable executive. The five logical planes—Elaina identity, AMFM, host learning, relationship and self-continuity—must remain distinguishable in authority, data and evaluation. They do not require five separately resident large models; base models may be shared with qualified adapters. Final choices remain empirical.

The successor fabric must retain raw evidence/archive, events and claims, graph and indexes, episodes, source/host/relationship/self memory, Mission Graph/workspace state, projection generations, retrieval/context fusion and workflow/model/dataset lineage. Alice must handle new events, contradictions, corrections, deletion, interrupted missions, restart and executive replacement with measurable continuity. The relevant experiments must demonstrate these behaviors; an import, heartbeat or successful training command cannot stand in for them.

At final acceptance, this qualified successor holds the declared operational authority, and Phase 2 serves its accepted compatibility/fallback/archive role. The user's memory and history are preserved through the transition.
