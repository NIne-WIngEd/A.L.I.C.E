**Gate dependency found in the final source review:** the existing P43 `qualify_n0_v02_full_envelope_gpu_memory_v1.py` computes `projection_pass` from a no-gradient forward plus a fixed fourfold activation multiplier and fails its receipt if any rank exceeds 85%. Both `verify_pre_gradient_runtime` and the training authorizer require that exact P43 PASS; the new whole-route receipt is checked *after* P43. The P43 code sets `ddp_boundary=complete_joint_step_v2` but still uses inference mode, so changing the outer DDP boundary does not turn its projected number into measured optimizer-step memory. Consequently, **neither the known P100 pair nor the observed T4 pair can pass the current authorization chain by merely running a new gradient diagnostic**. A cheaper full-step measurement by itself would be scientifically informative but could never authorize this unchanged training path. This is a contract design gap, not a reason to override a FAIL, alter the projection threshold, or spoof a P43 receipt.

The acceptable correction is a *versioned measured P43 successor* and its whole-route producer together: run the same complete topology and unchanged ten-family/18-pair stress objective, record per-rank first/later optimizer, accumulation, allocator and device peaks, require each actual peak under the original 85% policy, and bind its PASS and source hashes consistently through `verify_pre_gradient_runtime`, the authorizer, trainer and resume chain. Preserve the old inference projection as an independent conservative diagnostic and its failed receipts. Reconcile any source edit with fresh P42/static/CPU, P39PN and input audits. Only **after that complete contract exists** should a single bounded free-GPU attempt be considered. If free hardware still fails actual full-step capacity, close that route without a series of P43 retries. Paid high-memory hardware cannot repair this authorization defect either.

The proposed last qualification is **one provider-neutral full-step scientific experiment**, executed on a free service only after that service meets the hardware, source, data and runtime prerequisites *and the versioned measured gate above is implemented*. Its result must cover all 18 J3 stress pairs on both ranks, a finite joint loss and every active family, finite nonzero named gradients including the public judgment probe, actual per-rank allocated/reserved/device memory through backward and the *first and later* AdamW steps, full eight-microbatch accumulation, model/objective/optimizer/scheduler checkpoint, same-stage restart against an uninterrupted control, and predecessor J1→J2→J3 state loading. A paired case that fits in isolation does not establish the accumulated optimizer-step peak. Store rank-level receipts, environment/build hashes, failures and unchanged source/data hashes in a fresh evidence root. Do not turn diagnostic optimizer steps into a persisted production checkpoint or a DEV/FINAL result.

**Current blockers:** the candidate trainer explicitly requires `qualify_n0_v02_complete_joint_route_v2.py`, which has not yet been implemented, and the existing P43's inference projection cannot authorize the currently observed free GPU shapes even if a measured full step fits. Its absence deliberately prevents any full-route PASS or training authorization. There is no honest one-command Magnolia/Kaggle training qualification today. Implement and test the *whole* qualifier, refresh source-bound P42/static/CPU, public-mixture P39PN and dependency receipts at the final source revision, and check them against the actual allocated hardware before the single bounded run. The existing a19 mixture/source receipts cannot be relabeled for the new `171e0cc` head. A new qualifier or other scientific source edit changes that head again and requires a fresh exact-head chain. This prerequisite is identical for a paid host.

A new provider is **not inherently incompatible** with N0. The risks above are real and **currently unverified on any paid host**. A 2×80 GB A100 *class* offer is a conservative hardware shortlist, not a purchased or certified machine: 85% of 80 GB gives substantial headroom over the historical 17.75 GB projection, but this does not guarantee the full-step peak or a working runtime. Reject Blackwell MIG slices and one-card offers for this planned replica/DDP route unless the source and proofs are revised. Provider listings can vary in GPU count, per-card VRAM, CPU/RAM, image, storage, uptime and charge; no offer ID, account entitlement or current price has been verified in this record. Runpod and Vast explicitly expose configurable GPU/card and storage parameters in their own docs; compare an **actual available instance offer**, not a generic model name. [Runpod Pod selection](https://docs.runpod.io/pods/choose-a-pod) · [Vast offer fields](https://docs.vast.ai/guides/instances/choosing/find-and-rent).

**Decision rule:** Free route is closed under the old source/receipt chain. One last complete new-route test requires the versioned measured P43 and whole-route proof, plus fresh exact-head receipts; use free GPUs only if they actually allocate the requested shape. If neither does, obtain an exact two-GPU high-memory offer, fully review price and environment before approval, and recognize that its first runtime verification necessarily occurs after allocation. No source review can promise zero paid diagnostic minutes or guarantee model quality. Stop the paid allocation immediately on a package/driver/data hash or full-step failure and preserve the evidence. No purchase, image deployment, Kaggle repush or Magnolia GPU job has been authorized by this document.
