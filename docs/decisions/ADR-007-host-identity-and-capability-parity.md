# ADR-007 — Codename, Host-Selected Identity, and Full Capability Parity

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-07-27

## Decision

1. `Friday` is an internal product codename, not the fixed identity presented to every user.
2. Each host selects the assistant's local name and may evolve its voice and identity.
3. The public product brand is selected separately and does not define storage or model schemas.
4. The consumer product and A.L.I.C.E. share the same ultimate capability destination.
5. A.L.I.C.E. may lead experimental work; successful generalizable capabilities enter a parity ledger and downstream productization process.
6. A dedicated consumer team may later maintain the product while Rayan returns primary focus to A.L.I.C.E.

## Consequences

- Identity schemas must separate product, host, and assistant naming.
- Product validators must reject a permanent consumer capability ceiling.
- Roadmaps track productization lag rather than feature exclusion.
- A.L.I.C.E. personal state never enters consumer defaults.
- The shared kernel becomes the capability source of truth.
