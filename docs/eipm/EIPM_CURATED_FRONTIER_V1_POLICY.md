# EIPM Curated Frontier v1 Policy

**Status:** owner-directed working rule

## Curation posture

EIPM curation must preserve useful personality diversity rather than over-prune uncertain but plausible behavior.

A-SYN does not need to be historically proven. A useful A-SYN branch may remain provisionally curated when it is compatible with canonical E0, explicitly synthetic, revisable, non-autobiographical, and not contradicted by E0.

E-INF remains stricter because it is an evidence-derived inference about historical Elaina tendency. Direct canonical E0 support is required.

## Preferred actions

When a row has a repairable problem, prefer repair or reclassification over deletion:

- separate identity-supporting E0 from context-only E0 and excluded host/third-party context;
- reclassify a weak historical E-INF into A-SYN when the behavioral prior remains useful;
- repair obvious relationship/scenario template mismatches;
- factorize mechanical composition into semantic base policies plus relationship/state transforms;
- preserve plausible alternatives as competitors rather than automatically treating them as negatives;
- keep low-confidence but useful A-SYN as provisional instead of forcing a false binary accept/reject decision.

## Factorization rule

Mechanically expanded context variants are not independent personality evidence merely because they occupy separate rows. Preserve the semantic base policy and the transform blueprint separately. Validate or regenerate context-specific variants before they become training supervision.

## Provenance

E0 remains immutable.

Curated E-INF/A-SYN remain non-E0 and revisable. Host or third-party facts may condition behavior but may not silently become Elaina identity evidence.

## Training boundary

A curated frontier is still not final training data. No EIPM gradient update or target-scale weight generation is authorized until the corpus, architecture, tokenizer, and training configuration are frozen and the owner completes the final pre-weight review.
