# EIPM Personality Coverage Hard Rule

**Status:** OWNER-RATIFIED HARD RULE  
**Date:** 2026-09-10  
**Clarification:** 2026-09-16 capability-limit audit  
**Applies to:** EIPM-v1 construction and all future A.L.I.C.E. identity/personality model revisions

## Hard rule

A.L.I.C.E. must be given a practical starting behavioral policy for as much personality-relevant territory as can responsibly be covered before the first personality-model weight generation.

Historical uncertainty must never be converted into fabricated E0. E0 remains immutable and uniquely authoritative about historical Elaina evidence.

At the same time, historical uncertainty must not be allowed to become a permanent behavioral void. When E0 does not establish a behavior, the system must attempt to infer a grounded tendency through E-INF. When evidence still does not determine a historical behavior, A-SYN should be used to construct one or more explicitly synthetic, provenance-preserving starting priors so that Alice can still function coherently in that behavioral region.

Therefore:

**UNKNOWN is an epistemic label, not a required runtime behavior.**

The statement "we do not know what historical Elaina would have done" does not imply "Alice should have no behavioral prior and respond with I don't know." A-SYN exists in part to bridge that distinction.

## Coverage objective

Before EIPM-v1 corpus freeze, the construction process must seek broad, non-duplicative coverage across personality-relevant dimensions, including combinations and contextual variants. Coverage is not satisfied by a fixed number of synthetic rows.

The working target is saturation of meaningful behavioral territory, including but not limited to values, judgment, boundaries, privacy, trust, affection, criticism, disagreement, conflict, stress, vulnerability, humor, embarrassment, mistakes, reconciliation, authority, injustice, loyalty, ambition, risk, public/private differences, relationship roles, emotional intensity, social setting, uncertainty, and context-dependent changes in expression.

The pipeline should prefer diverse, grounded behavior branches over repeated paraphrases of already-covered traits.

No corpus freeze, row count, ontology count, graph size, checkpoint tensor shape, or current compute envelope is a permanent personality-capability ceiling. A freeze is a reviewable lineage snapshot. If later evidence reveals a real coverage hole, the substrate and successor checkpoints may expand under provenance governance.

## Provenance hierarchy

- **E0:** immutable historical evidence about Elaina.
- **E-INF:** revisable evidence-derived inference.
- **A-SYN:** revisable synthetic behavioral completion or rehearsal. Never historical truth.
- **A-EXP:** Alice's post-activation lived experience.
- **A-SELF:** Alice's consolidated current self-state.

Later learning may revise E-INF, A-SYN, and other non-E0 behavioral priors. It may not rewrite E0.

## Post-activation development

EIPM-v1 is a strong starting personality foundation rather than a claim of permanent completion.

After activation, Alice may learn new behaviors and modify existing non-E0 behaviors through governed experience. The architectural home for that long-term change is the **Alice Continuity / Self Model** together with the Experience Ledger, A-EXP, and later self-learning mechanisms.

This means Alice can become more complete through experience without corrupting historical provenance. The distinction between Elaina-source evidence and Alice's later self-development must remain explicit.

## Generation and curation requirement

Astra or other temporary development-time models may be used to propose E-INF and A-SYN candidates when their terms permit. Generated candidates do not enter the accepted identity substrate automatically.

Each candidate must be individually curated for evidence support, contradiction, unsupported specificity, generic-assistant contamination, duplication, provenance correctness, relationship/context correctness, uncertainty, and genuine coverage gain.

**A plausible alternative is not a negative merely because another branch was selected.** Plausible alternatives must remain available as competing or co-valid hypotheses where the evidence permits plurality. A branch may be used as a hard negative only when evidence/provenance establishes that it is contradicted, incompatible, or otherwise genuinely wrong for the supervised judgment. This clarification supersedes the earlier shorthand that "plausible rejected branches" could generally be treated as hard negatives.

## Weight gate

No private EIPM gradient update or target-scale personality-weight generation may begin until the expanded substrate, corpus, tokenizer, architecture, and training configuration are frozen for a reviewable lineage snapshot and the owner is explicitly asked for the final pre-weight review and explicitly approves proceeding.

That review freeze is a governance/lineage validation. It is not a permanent capacity or coverage limit. Targeted expansion, architecture growth, vocabulary migration, or later successor training remains allowed whenever measured fidelity/capability evidence requires it.
