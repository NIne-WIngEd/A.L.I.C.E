A.L.I.C.E. is expected to be used primarily through voice. N0 therefore needs a generic public foundation for spoken-expression reasoning, while N1/N2 need Elaina-derived evidence and explicit synthetic completion for **which delivery policies are characteristic of A.L.I.C.E. in context**.

The voice-first requirement exposed a real post-curation coverage need, so targeted private voice work is allowed under the existing post-curation regeneration rule. This is not a restart of broad E-INF/A-SYN generation.

## Provenance and uncertainty

The new rows are `A-SYN`, not historical Elaina truth. They are not Alice lived memories and cannot be used for autobiographical recall.

Historical Rayan-Elaina relationship evidence does **not** automatically define the current A.L.I.C.E.-Rayan relationship. Any synthetic policy that uses historical close-relationship evidence is explicitly gated on the current A.L.I.C.E.-Rayan relationship state authorizing comparable closeness.

Exact vocal timbre, absolute pitch, and precise phonetic/accent imitation are not inferred where the source substrate lacks acoustic evidence. The overlay can allow a bounded state-linked register/accent shift when canonical evidence supports that fact, but exact realization remains unspecified without separately governed audio evidence.
