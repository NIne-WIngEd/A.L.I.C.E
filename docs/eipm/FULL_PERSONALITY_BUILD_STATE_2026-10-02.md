## Implementation and evidence still required

N1 must learn the governed source concepts, conditional policy/evidence graph,
source authority and auditable residual nuance. It must distinguish immutable
source history, revisable inference, synthetic behavior, current host and
relationship, and Alice's actual later self. A concept count report is not N1.

N2 must learn context-conditioned candidate preferences and the complete
multi-dimensional IDP: stance, value conflicts, relational/emotional posture,
communication and voice intent, uncertainty/plurality, evidence support,
contraindications, drift and latent identity. Candidate order cannot define the
target. A plausible unselected alternative is not a negative; each contradiction
or incompatibility needs source grounding. A scalar ranker cannot replace IDP.

N3 must calibrate uncertainty, margins and failure tails and incorporate
owner-reviewed full-response fidelity without rewriting historical authority.
Complete spoken behavior and expression must survive downstream provider
replacement. Text-only mechanics do not qualify this requirement.

The actual producer needs versioned implemented ACFP/IDP schemas and codecs,
source-family and generator separation, permission/source/loss masks,
stage-specific losses, downstream-only optimization, reproducible optimizer/RNG
and resume, calibrated exports and reload, independent behavior evaluation and
correction/deletion influence checks. The retired custom-N0 special IDs,
17-state/640-width geometry and upstream gradient policy do not apply to Gemma.

Before real teaching compilation, harden the old compiler: strict boolean
authority fields, complete manifest/file hash membership, safe append-only
extraction/output, resolved inference lineage and split groups that close shared
source-support and transform dependencies. UNKNOWN is epistemic uncertainty,
not a behavioral void; ASYN supplies explicit governed starting priors.

## Independent public semantics

Human-annotated FewRel TRAIN/DEV is the strongest independent relation-target
source found in the old public plan. The old materializer **and auditor** open
FINAL data; they cannot be reused unchanged. Prefer already isolated receipted
TRAIN/DEV artifacts, or a new extraction route that never opens FINAL sentences
or descriptions. Transparent synthetic intervention targets and service-teacher
semantics can supplement teaching, with explicit provenance and independent
checks. Generic helper, voice or relationship preferences are not identity gold.

Public forward fixtures exercise execution. Source-family holdouts, natural
relation targets, source attribution, relevant/irrelevant interventions and
semantic regressions must separately test suitability for identity learning.
Inherited preferences must be localized and repaired in first-party readouts or
teaching, without silent upstream edits or broad removal of useful semantics.
