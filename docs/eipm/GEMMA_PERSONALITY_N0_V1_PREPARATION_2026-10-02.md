## Why this model defines Alice

Alice's identity component owns interpretation, priorities, judgment,
relationship-sensitive behavior and expression. It must preserve that character
when the language generator, feature provider, or speech renderer changes.
Fluent imitation, an agreeable assistant, a recalled biography, or a personality
prompt cannot satisfy the objective.

Alice reconstructs an evidence-grounded Elaina foundation while keeping Rayan
host state, their relationship and Alice's post-activation self distinct. General
Fable builds a different owner's personal foundation and a distinct assistant,
not an idealized clone of that owner. Supported disagreement and characteristic
care must come from the personal path; a general model's defaults cannot supply
the answer in an uncovered region and be relabeled personal learning.

Source authority remains separate: E0 is direct historical evidence, E-INF is
revisable inference, A-SYN is explicit behavioral completion, and A-EXP/A-SELF
refer to post-activation experience/self. UNKNOWN preserves historical
uncertainty; it does not require a behavioral void. Starting behavioral priors
must be explicitly governed A-SYN, not concealed Gemma or teacher preferences.

## What N0 must preserve and exclude

N0 supplies general semantic, pragmatic, relational, evidential, temporal,
uncertainty and candidate-comparison competence. It must retain token-level
context, evidence wording and relations so later identity layers can make
query-conditioned judgments. A whole-document mean vector or a generated answer
is not a sufficient personality interface.

The v1 preparation route therefore constructs a **features-only role interface**
around the receipted source. It loads safely from local files, freezes the
backbone in evaluation mode, invokes the representation path and returns every
actual text hidden layer, final states and their validated attention mask. It
retains the original full-envelope access to depth rather than making final
states a permanent capability ceiling. Gemma's dimensions and tokenizer govern
this adapter; the retired custom-N0 layer/width assumptions do not. It does not expose
Gemma logits, generation, a chat template or a Gemma-selected stance. No
upstream gradients, tensor replacement, MFM decoder or formation target is part
of this route. A separate fresh personal readout will own identity decisions.

This is an architectural suppression of the obvious generative output path.
It is not evidence that the hidden representations are unbiased. Semantic
representations necessarily carry pretrained influence. The objective is to
prevent inherited preferences from controlling identity choices while retaining
the general understanding the personal layers need. A validated loader and
unchanged weights alone cannot prove that.

The preparation record binds source/role custody, implementation bytes and exact
runtime versions. It remains `PREPARED_UNQUALIFIED`. It cannot be advertised as
a neutral checkpoint or used as a substitute for N1-N3.

## Personality-specific build sequence

1. **Prepare N0.** Rehash the actual source and separate personality role copy;
   construct the features-only interface; verify complete nontruncated input,
   freezing, finite representations and actual target-runtime forward behavior.
   Public probe examples exercise the route, not competence or neutrality.
2. **Qualify N0 for identity learning.** Use independent public semantic,
   pragmatics, relations, source attribution, candidate comparison and
   voice-expression interpretation cases. Preserve evidence families and source
   splits. The old 43-competency and public voice requirements are a coverage
   starting point, not proof that the new Gemma route passed them. A fresh public
   task readout may be learned if a demonstrated interface gap requires it; do
   not recreate the custom-N0 pretraining project.
3. **N1: identity representation.** Learn explicit evidence-supported identity
   concepts, their support/context/exclusion relations, conditional tendencies
   and bounded residual nuance from the authorized identity substrate. Preserve
   E0/E-INF/A-SYN, uncertainty and source-family separation. Host preferences
   must not replace the source identity. Do not import MFM proposal weights.
4. **N2: personal judgment and expression.** Learn context-conditioned candidate
   preference and a multi-dimensional decision packet: stance, competing values,
   relationship/emotional posture, uncertainty, evidence, out-of-character
   signals and expressive intent. Co-valid alternatives are not automatically
   negative labels. Joint wording/delivery policy matters; EIPM is not a TTS
   waveform model. A scalar reward or a memory proposal cannot replace this.
5. **N3: calibration and fidelity.** Check uncertainty, margins, failure tails,
   generic-assistant/sycophancy drift and owner-reviewed source fidelity.
   Voice-ready promotion includes complete spoken response review. Calibration
   cannot rewrite historical authority or turn a simulated outcome into a
   lived event.
6. **Integrated evidence and export.** Bind actual ACFP/IDP interfaces only when
   the producer implements and tests them. Native judgment precedes feature
   generation; locally check both verdict and expression. Export the actual
   host-specific learned package with all required files and lineage. N0 alone
   has no personality verdict to export.

Steps 2-6 remain unrun. This branch implements step 1's software boundary and a
public forward diagnostic. It does not claim learned N1-N3 or approve N0.

## Detect and repair inherited influence

Before training-based selection or final evaluation, freeze the case families,
splits, metrics and decision thresholds. Use paired interventions that isolate
the intended cause:

- hold evidence and general answer quality fixed; change governed identity
  preferences and require the learned personal choice to change;
- hold identity fixed; vary generic helpful, therapeutic or flattering wording,
  candidate order, names, formatting and irrelevant host changes; the supported
  personal verdict must remain stable;
- provide relevant source, host, relationship or self changes one at a time;
  only appropriate dimensions change, and source-person history stays distinct;
- preserve genuine semantic differences, such as sarcasm, privacy boundaries,
  intensity and evidence uncertainty; do not remove these as mere style noise;
- swap language providers and reject a correct conclusion expressed with the
  wrong personal voice; remove/stale the learned identity state and verify the
  same personality is not recreated by a hidden prompt;
- compare against an unmodified source control and independent fresh readout
  control; compare retained-personality and updated-personality paths under the
  same selected evidence. Expose failures instead of assuming frozen is neutral.

When a concrete inherited preference survives these controls, localize it to
input packaging, representation/readout, training targets or downstream
expression. Repair the first-party projection/readout or targeted training
material; rerun both that counterfactual and semantic regressions. Do not choose
blind tensor surgery, blanket helpfulness removal, or new permanent capability
ceilings. No empirical inherited-influence result exists yet.
