## Research adopted and limits

[RolePersonality](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.18921v2) distinguishes character knowledge/tone from personality and motivation, and constructs both single-turn and multi-turn material. Adopt the need to elicit motives, choices and continuity. Its questionnaire types are discovery prompts, not a personality ontology, identity gold or a reason to use an external teacher. Its synthetic distillation, stereotypes and automated judge do not transfer to this owner-designated primary-teacher build.

[CharacterBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.11912v1) shows why sparse features need situations that actually elicit the dimension being measured. Adopt dimension-relevant scene construction and distinguish attributes, behavior, emotion and memory consistency. Its character-prompt generator, general moral score, human-likeness score and CharacterJudge cannot replace ALICE's provenance, individual values or clone awareness. [PersonaGym](https://personagym.com/) separates expected actions, action justification, habits and consistency across environments; this supports covering actions and reasons beyond stylistic replies, not adopting its external judge ensemble.

[Counterfactual augmentation](https://arxiv.org/html/1909.12434) motivates mixing original teaching with coherent changed-condition cases. [Joshi and He](https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.256/) establish that narrowly edited augmentation can suppress useful unedited features and amplify replacement shortcuts. Adopt diverse conditions and constructions, not repeated permission withdrawal. These papers do not establish this model's unique failure cause, optimum data size, loss weights or architecture.

[Experience replay](https://arxiv.org/html/1902.10486v4) jointly teaches new examples with retained old examples and measures forgetting. Adopt cumulative reviewed replay with prior-capability checks. Its image benchmarks provide no ALICE replay ratio or buffer ceiling. [REMIND's primary implementation](https://github.com/tyler-hayes/REMIND) supplies a frozen-feature/plastic-component precedent; do not import its lossy feature compression, CNN geometry or identity claims. Reuse exact verified feature banks only for unchanged texts and provider bindings.

[PersonaMem](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.14225v2) separates recalling preferences, tracking their changes, retaining reasons and applying current preferences in a new situation. Adopt those distinct teaching situations. Its synthetic user-personalization data does not establish Alice's own personality, and its incorrect multiple-choice options do not license rejecting co-valid ALICE alternatives. [LongMemEval's primary implementation](https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval) distinguishes extraction, multi-session reasoning, updates, temporal reasoning and abstention. These help identify continuity lessons; they do not justify replacing MFM or treating a memory-QA score as personality fidelity. [LoCoMo](https://snap-research.github.io/locomo/) motivates causal/temporal event continuity beyond isolated turns. Rehearsals remain A-SYN; actual persistence, retrieval and outcome changes must be demonstrated through their owning interfaces.

The current frontier-watch intake at `db910c3c9d2917f1d0b628f880686ce8195fe2c4` was consulted. Its source/task-binding and exposure-versus-utility lessons reinforce causal use of current evidence. They do not justify importing a memory platform or changing the selected personality architecture. Older custom-N0 readiness queues remain superseded by the owner's frozen-Gemma v1 decision.
