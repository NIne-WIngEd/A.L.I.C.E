## The next concrete seam

Source compilation preserves facts, conditions, permissions and lineage. It
does not turn them into independently reviewed conditional judgments. The next
substantive interface is a versioned source-linked teaching-case document and
deterministic named-pointer materializer:

`verified source facts + separately reviewed cases -> target-free complete
CognitiveFrameDocument -> closed qualified feature producer -> exact sparse
ReviewedTarget tensors + TeachingBatch -> reviewed teaching admission`.

Keep row eligibility, case annotation, package acceptance, N0 suitability and
current gradient authorization separate. Existing receipts remain immutable;
new input/target documents and producer sessions need their own bindings. This
is a construction requirement, not an added user approval flow.

## Case and target facts to materialize

Each case needs exact source/compiler/protocol/annotation pins, connected source
families, reviewed split allocation, annotation origin and review evidence.
Its complete frame preserves governing facts, selected spans, applicability and
disconfirmation conditions, temporal scope and missing observations. Preferred
indices and case-specific gold rationale stay outside input text.

Keep neutral lawful candidates, including characteristic and uncharacteristic
choices. Preserve initiative, clarification, disagreement, repair and deferral
when relevant. Candidate IDs and order are pointers, not semantic inputs.
Targets name exact candidate/source/concept/label pointers, values, availability,
annotation uncertainty, support/disconfirmation and review lineage. Specify
whether the reviewed comparison is closed or partial. An unselected or unknown
alternative is not automatically negative; plurality is not a forced winner.

The materializer must reject duplicate/unknown pointers and unauthorized losses,
preserve unavailable targets and NaN placeholders, and bind exact document,
feature and target bytes after producer closure. It must not interpret free-text
conditions, source confidence or personality dimensions as automatic gold.
Compiler train/dev/test and teaching TRAIN/DEV/CALIBRATION_TRAIN/CALIBRATION_DEV
need an explicit reviewed allocation bridge preserving support-family closure;
no automatic test-to-FINAL renaming is justified.

## Three semantic limits requiring explicit treatment

`teaching/objectives.py::_distribution` normalizes only over target availability.
A singleton reviewed positive consequently has zero conditional cross entropy,
regardless of other eligible sources. This can be a valid partial annotation,
but it is not discrimination evidence. Record closed reviewed comparison sets
and effective discrimination-bearing coverage; do not invent rival negatives.

Current `contrast_loss` rewards absolute output separation for relevant changes.
A wrong-direction change can satisfy that distance. Conditional paired cases
need independently reviewed consequences or directional targets, alongside
named-dimension invariance for irrelevant pressure.

Evidence pointers normalize over eligible sources. Eligibility does not mean
relevance: an unrelated authorized source may retain mass after decisive support
is removed. Until a separately reviewed support/abstention contract exists,
pointers rank potential evidence and are not citation authority. Teach explicit
insufficiency/uncertainty where reviewed and leave unsupported pointer targets
unavailable. A direct no-support output or independent relevance probabilities
would require an explicit schema/objective migration; no threshold is invented.

## Safest public mechanism demonstration

A separately authored fictional subject can have conditional rules for private
versus public critique, consent/low stakes versus distress, and permitted humor.
Keep candidate sets fixed; independently review contextual preference changes,
plural choices, irrelevant host pressure, decisive-support removal and synthetic
policy versus historical claims. These cases need genuine annotation review
before semantic claims. They are not Alice/Elaina biography or owner fidelity.

Compare source/metadata/graph/candidate-only controls, correctness and justified
direction, invariance with retained correctness, source sufficiency and expression
coherence. Connected paraphrases/derivatives remain in the same split family.
Public fictional learning can demonstrate a conditional mechanism; it cannot
qualify ALICE identity, full voice, source acceptance or learned FBM transfer.

Private production still requires actual lineage closure, suitable personality
N0 and reviewed complete teaching inputs/targets. No governing learning recipe
is adopted by this review. N1 conditional teaching, N2 full sparse judgments and
separate N3 calibration must be substantive capabilities, not auxiliary scores.
