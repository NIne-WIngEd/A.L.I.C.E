## Teaching admission and execution

Corrections come from existing independently admitted TRAIN N1 cases for the
same documents as the80 broader N2 cases. A separate correction schedule binds
the exact case/document/frame, N1 batch/review record, all available fact values,
annotation uncertainty and evidence pointers, field/pointer coordinates, and
the unchanged N2 target batch. Diagnostic outputs never become teaching data.
Unavailable facts and facts with annotation uncertainty1 are excluded. No new
teacher labels or confidence interpolation is invented. Partial probes select
complete concept groups deterministically; they remain diagnostic.

Both arms start from original9f3de2, with frozen Gemma/N1/N3 and frozen objective
parameters. Same40 groups,216 cases, fresh AdamW, original learning rate .0003,
clip1, same RNG seed and one pass. Each endpoint accumulates half its complete
native loss before and half after the replay; clipping occurs once per group.
Other136 cases receive unchanged replay in both arms. The teacher hook is scoped
to training and restoration is mandatory. No persistent model architecture or
ordinary inference hook changes. Native N2 projections are never cached across
updates. Each completed optimizer group is saved with exact model, optimizer,
RNG and event history under a distinct experimental schema.

After demonstrated natural conditional judgment and retention, continue the
broader N1/N2 learning connection, N3 outcome calibration and typed consumers
through existing Memory/MFM, Mission/Workspace and Experience contracts. Full
38-family/192-facet coverage remains the goal. This experiment cannot qualify
the whole personality model or Gemma N0. FBM captures the construction procedure
and evidence without private identity payload or learned-builder claims.
