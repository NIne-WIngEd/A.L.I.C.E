## Relevant primary mechanisms

* [Contrast Sets](https://aclanthology.org/2020.findings-emnlp.117.pdf) use author-validated local meaning changes and consistency across all members, including the original. Useful here: freeze candidate wording, change only the decisive condition and review changed gold before outputs. These small local sets do not establish whole-role or population accuracy.
* [CheckList](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.442.pdf) separates minimum functionality, invariance and directional tests. Its [expectation implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/marcotcr/checklist/master/checklist/expect.py) and [test types](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/marcotcr/checklist/master/checklist/test_types.py) make these different claims explicit. Adopt the comparison design, not its default 0.1 tolerance, large test matrix, dependencies or threshold policy. A constant wrong prediction must not pass our invariance claim.
* [Persona Vectors](https://www.anthropic.com/research/persona-vectors) validates behavior changes through causal interventions and reports capability costs of some post-hoc steering. Its [actual extractor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/safety-research/persona_vectors/main/generate_vec.py) uses generated prompt-plus-response activations and judged trait/coherence filters. Its [evaluation code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/safety-research/persona_vectors/main/eval/eval_persona.py) uses generated responses and external judges. That machinery is incompatible with assuming this headless non-IT Gemma already has an identified removable direction. Use behavioral contrasts; do not import vectors, judges, upstream hooks or coefficients.
* [Assistant Axis](https://www.anthropic.com/research/assistant-axis) found related persona structure in tested base and post-trained models. This supports checking residual pretrained associations, not claiming neutrality from non-IT provenance. Its assistant-stabilization objective and tested model families differ from ALICE's separate personal judgment. No tested result proves interference in this exact Gemma pin.

Frontier-watch's October 9 DPPM intake is an optional future parametric-memory challenger, not a repair for current N0. It does not justify changing the present architecture or restarting a foundation program.
