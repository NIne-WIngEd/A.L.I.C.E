  with learned gated cross-attention. Its initialization argument fits the
  preserved-base constraint, but its data and task do not establish ALICE fidelity.

- [LLaMA-Adapter](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.16199v3) supports zero-gated added
  routes with the original model frozen; neither its teacher data nor its

- [Side-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.13503) motivates an additive learned
  branch over a fixed base. Fixed original weights do not guarantee unchanged
  active behavior; full retention must still be measured.
