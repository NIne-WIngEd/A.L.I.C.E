## Mandatory diagnosis: the observed governing boundary

1. **Validate evidence.** A deliberately conflicting public example used 120 actual N2 optimizer steps, a real optimized checkpoint, the actual consumer and a captured renderer input. Preference favored the selected candidate (0.98198), while native co-validity was 0.00022, contraindication 0.99990, uncertainty 0.88597 and evidence sufficiency 0.10485. The consumer still marked it acceptable and sent only six reduced intent keys. This proves propagation/conflict visibility, not private identity quality or a risk threshold. The original diagnostic is retained.
2. **Connect the claim to the goal.** Multidimensional identity judgment must govern the actual response. A preference-only handoff can erase governing information even if the model emits it correctly. Full candidate construction is necessary but insufficient.
3. **Trace architecture.** Full source-bound frame → N1 representation → contextual N2 → separate N3 → native IDP → `DecisionConsumer.respond` → optional renderer. Complete `decision_to_mapping` already preserves the native verdict. Existing conversation and Experience paths are separately implemented but not connected to this personality path.
4. **Locate departure.** `identity/consumer.py:146–159` builds a reduced intent and unconditionally includes chosen candidates among acceptable candidates. Lines 162–175 pass that reduced intent to the renderer. This is a downstream implementation issue independent of native model correctness.
5. **Preserve responsibility.** The consumer binds actual source/document/frame/checkpoint state, retains co-validity and plurality, and checks realized words/delivery against the personal choice. Preserve those guarantees and source/subject ownership. A new response helper must use the actual native consumer and packet, never a reconstructed teacher score.
6. **Coherent correction.** Carry the complete bound native decision and explicit teaching scope to a replaceable renderer. Distinguish the original preferred choice from acceptable behavior; preserve unresolved conflict when the selected choice falls outside the consumer's existing taught co-valid region. Do not invent measured risk guarantees, hidden owner approval or an alternative ranking policy.
7. **Research and applicability.** Original role doctrine rejects a single scalar as full personality judgment. Existing codec and consumer tests supply the implementable mechanism. [Selective classification](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/file/4a8423d5e91fda00bb7e46540e2b0cf1-Paper.pdf) and its [primary risk-control implementation](https://github.com/geifmany/selective_deep_learning/blob/master/risk_control.py) separate ranking/confidence from a selection rule; their empirical guarantees require suitable independently observed errors. Those assumptions are absent here, so no calibrated-risk threshold or guarantee is imported. [PAL's frozen-foundation training path](https://github.com/RamyaLab/pluralistic-alignment/blob/main/main_pal_b_fix_llm.py) supports modular personal learning but its reward task does not replace our full IDP. Current frontier intake reinforces admissibility versus relevance, current applicability versus historical success, and real outcome versus incomplete process evidence.
8. **Verify intended correction.** The continuation plan specifies actual optimized public controls for conflicting preference/validity, full verdict propagation across replaceable renderers, unchanged personal words, explicit untaught scope and bound packet preservation. Actual private full-chain recovery and substantive governing fit remain separate evidence. Implementation and verification results will be recorded with their limits; no full personality claim follows from these fixtures.

## The additional actual teaching issue

Root's authorized primary review compared retained pre/post N3 decisions with the existing reviewed outcome, preserving the original source/context and annotations. The permission-change case has provisional primary targets co-validity 0.05 and contraindication 0.95; actual post-N3 values remain 0.98565 and 0.00036. Native N3 scales those N2 logits by a strictly positive factor before sigmoid, so it cannot cross 0.5 when their signs are wrong. Head temperatures likewise cannot reverse the preferred categorical label. Its contextual risk readout can learn uncertainty/failure/fidelity when legitimate observations exist; it is not the governing re-judgment stage.

This validates a stage-responsibility limit and an unresolved governing fit problem. It does not yet isolate whether the responsible N1/N2 issue is conditional teaching coverage, target meaning, input use, objective or capacity. Preserve these outcomes and finish that primary diagnosis before another learning run. Correcting the consumer alone cannot repair wrong native judgments. No N3 retry, capacity increase, arbitrary threshold or calibration-to-TRAIN relabeling is authorized by this observation.

The original reviewed TRAIN targets were then compared with the saved complete decisions, by exact document and named target pointer. N1 conditional applicability matches the original binary region on 63/80 available facts (mean absolute error 0.22867); N2 co-validity matches 119/120 (0.01507), and contraindication matches 118/120 (0.01973). These are development target-fit measurements, not evaluation. In the first two thematic pairs, N1's policy applicability barely changes when the teaching target changes from one to zero. N2 nevertheless fits the original choices. The implementation already passes N1 conditional signals into N2; their causal use is not established by that connection or by training fit.

The initial N1 package teaches provenance, applicability and source support, with no reviewed conditional uncertainty or disconfirmation targets. The original paired cases change present conditions and candidate wording together. They therefore cannot isolate whether N2 follows present conditions or recognizable candidate wording. There is also a semantic distinction to settle in the primary protocol: a whole conditional policy that includes stopping when asked remains a governing rule when its playful behavior is inactive. Do not silently treat policy relevance and permission to perform its expressive behavior as the same annotation.

The next diagnostic is bounded to that responsible conditional path, using the actual saved candidate and source-bound cases. Establish the meaning of the primary target and distinguish current-context use from candidate wording before changing teaching. [Joshi and He's primary analysis](https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.256/) shows why narrowly varied counterfactual data can leave other robust features unlearned or introduce new shortcuts; [Geirhos et al.'s original code](https://github.com/rgeirhos/shortcut-perspective) demonstrates why familiar-example fit is insufficient. These mechanisms motivate the discriminating comparison; they do not prove this candidate's cause or justify a new backbone, automatic data expansion or a parameter search.
