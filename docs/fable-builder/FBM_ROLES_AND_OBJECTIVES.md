# FBM roles and objectives

**Updated:** October 15, 2026. **Status:** owner-requested direction, not capability certification.

FBM must build, connect, evaluate and maintain the complete personal foundation.
Its responsibility continues after the first model build. Backbone maintenance
belongs within that continuing builder role; a dedicated handler model is an
optional future specialization that must justify its additional cost.

This is the consolidated responsibility register. It does not select new weights,
storage engines or deployment topology, change PE/MFM workstream ownership, or
authorize automatic production changes. Each role below needs its own evidence.

## 1. Construction objectives

| ID | FBM must do | Evidence needed to meet the objective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FBM-01 | **Authorize and plan source intake.** Inventory original sources; offer eligible public packs and owner choices; distinguish processing, training, distribution and egress permissions; check source and hardware adequacy. | Reproducible source/rights/ancestry manifest; excluded or denied sources stay excluded; inadequate builds name the missing capability instead of pretending success. |
| FBM-02 | **Interpret and attribute evidence.** Preserve exact source spans or native modality references, speaker, subject, audience, time, quotation, uncertainty and contradictions. | Held-out wrong-speaker, quotation, temporal and modality cases retain correct attribution; unsupported interpretations remain unresolved. |
| FBM-03 | **Construct grounded identity hypotheses and starting policies.** Generate conditional alternatives, counterevidence and explicit synthetic completion; curate duplicates, unsupported specifics, generic-assistant contamination and sycophancy. | Source-person inference remains distinguishable from synthetic policy, host evidence and lived self-history; useful uncertainty is preserved without fabricating autobiography. |
| FBM-04 | **Build learning material.** Map coverage; choose curricula, hard cases, counterfactuals and negatives; preserve generator/source families and successful, failed, deferred and rejected alternatives. | Eligible input/target/outcome cases, not only procedure summaries; split independence, rights and leakage checks; targeted material closes a demonstrated capability gap. |
| FBM-05 | **Construct the required personal models.** Assemble, train/adapt where authorized, export and connect personality, MFM, host, relationship and assistant-self capabilities using each role's actual contract. | Traceable inputs, weight ancestry and complete consumer inference; each capability passes its own independent behavioral checks and contributes causally to the connected entity. |
| FBM-06 | **Construct supporting personal cognition.** Build the needed recollection/routing/ranking, context fusion, temporal/conflict interpretation, episode formation, consolidation, procedural and perceptual mechanisms. Include world/social/causal/trust/value/mission/prediction state and their required learned operators. | Each declared mechanism has an actual producer/consumer, grounded state and objective; demonstrate its contribution rather than counting a stored label or a new model name. |
| FBM-07 | **Assemble the full backbone.** Wire the logical planes listed below, their qualified adapters and their correction, deletion, recovery and authority links into one system. | A complete source-to-judgment-to-outcome-to-revision path survives the relevant restart, stale-state, contradiction, revocation and isolation cases. Individual backend passes are insufficient. |
| FBM-08 | **Connect judgment to expression and action.** Preserve the full native decision/uncertainty/expression packet, authorized feature execution, conversation handoff and independently observed outcomes. | State interventions change relevant verdict and expression; unrelated changes do not. Fluent but identity-inconsistent drafts are rejected; provider substitution does not silently replace native judgment. |
| FBM-09 | **Execute reproducible builds on available hardware.** Choose placement, scheduling, batching, precision, offload, storage and approved remote compute without deleting required capabilities. Preserve resume, export and artifact custody. | Measured complete-path resource use and continuation equivalence; a reproducible build manifest and honest unsupported-capability report. A successful launch or small smoke test is not a completed build. |
| FBM-10 | **Qualify the assembled result.** Run independent component, connected, causal, fidelity and transfer checks; report failures, uncertainty and limitations with each candidate. | Frozen acceptance criteria before final results; held-out people/source/generator families; independent targets and actual test execution. Builder-generated scores alone cannot authorize a release. |

### Personal models FBM must build and connect

- **Personality:** evidence-grounded conditional judgment, values, stance,
  uncertainty and expression; historical source-person evidence stays separate
  from later personal development.
- **MFM:** source-bound memory, claim, episode, goal, contradiction, correction
  and development proposals. Independent authority decides acceptance.
- **Host:** a revisable understanding of the user's preferences, beliefs,
  habits, goals, projects and context; a stated aspiration is evidence.
- **Relationship:** shared history, trust, norms, expectations and coordination,
  without overwriting either subject.
- **Assistant-self:** post-activation continuity, evaluations of prior choices,
  learned boundaries and changing behavior; not rewritten source-person history.

These are starting responsibilities, not a fixed neural-model count. Supporting
roles in FBM-06 are required when the full architecture needs them; choose learned
or deterministic implementations from evidence. Future native conversation,
reasoning or feature-model construction follows its own selected build plan.
Current feature providers remain replaceable tools. This document does not make
building frontier feature models a prerequisite for the present personal core.

### Backbone coverage required by FBM-07

- Encrypted original evidence/object custody; replayable Experience/Event history;
  accepted temporal Claims, proposals, dependencies and provenance.
- Episodes/autobiographical memory; cognitive multi-graph and associative views;
  vector/multimodal and exact/source-native/live retrieval; grounded perceptual memory.
- Host, relationship, self and the other cognitive state in FBM-06; parametric
  memory with training/influence lineage; working/activation memory and caches.
- Mission Graph goals, dependencies, decisions, actions and outcomes; Cognitive
  Workspace projections; procedural skills and linked learning episodes.
- Memory Resource Manager, Retrieval Orchestrator/Context Planner and Lifecycle
  Curator; scheduling, consolidation and storage tiers.
- Authority/consent boundaries, transport and durable workflows, model/data
  generations, cross-layer correction/deletion/unlearning response, backup/export,
  recovery and the full product's multi-device/federation semantics.

These are semantic obligations, not one service or database per bullet. Preserve
the latest owner-approved physical decision for the workstream; historical engine
tables do not override it. The bounded FloRA slice does not certify every plane.

## 2. Continuing responsibilities after the initial build

| ID | FBM must do | Evidence needed to meet the objective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FBM-11 | **Support governed personal development.** Diagnose learning gaps and propose dataset, component or training changes from authorized experience and verified outcomes. Keep normal memory proposals with MFM and live judgment with the personal roles. | Relevant later judgment changes for the supported reason; irrelevant controls stay stable; host/relationship/self separation, historical identity and update lineage survive. |
| FBM-12 | **Inspect data flow and localize failures.** Use permitted code/version snapshots, linked records, actual model inputs/outputs and independent observations. Distinguish test, data, model, backbone, adapter, provider and environment faults. | Correct localization on unseen failures and healthy controls; calibrated uncertainty, false-alarm and missed-defect reporting. Existing records do not imply complete telemetry. |
| FBM-13 | **Repair and improve the backbone and connected components.** Propose coherent changes to code, representations, retrieval/context construction, procedures, adapters and learning methods using the diagnosis sequence below. | An independently reproduced defect is corrected; affected contracts and broader regressions pass; compare benefit and resource cost against the unchanged version. No blind patch stack or weakened evaluator. |
| FBM-14 | **Prepare controlled replacement and lifecycle work.** Package model/code/data migrations, correction/deletion responses and recovery plans; supply promotion evidence and inspect post-change outcomes. | Separately authorized activation; compatible state migration; demonstrated recovery and continuity. Code rollback alone cannot undo a migration, disclosure or external action. |
| FBM-15 | **Learn better construction methods.** Capture compact observable operations and later results; turn eligible cases into transferable builder competence; propose improvements to FBM itself under the same independent controls. | Gains on unseen build/repair families and people, including retained failures and no-change cases; keep private identity and gradients out of shared weights and verify eligibility of reusable methods separately. Trace counts are not learning. |
| FBM-16 | **Reproduce the personal foundation for another host.** Transfer validated methods and qualified neutral components, then build from the new host's authorized evidence. Maintain local ownership and continuing update/recovery ability. | Different evidence produces appropriately different entities; no cross-host private state; sparse evidence stays uncertain; no paid teacher or expert labeling dependency at installation; required capabilities remain connected. |

Continuing responsibility means FBM remains available for governed work. It does
not require continuous residency, an inference call on every request, or a
particular timer. Maintenance runs outside ordinary serving with explicit resource
and permission boundaries. A separate specialist is justified only by a matched
comparison showing better verified repairs or cost with acceptable regressions.

## 3. Authority and mandatory diagnosis

1. Validate the failing test or evidence before treating it as a system defect.
2. State its intended claim and importance to the whole project.
3. Trace the sound test through the responsible architecture.
4. Locate the first supported failure; resolve uncertainty with a targeted diagnostic.
5. Understand that component's purpose, guarantees and connections.
6. Propose a coherent correction and affected checks; preserve the original failure.
7. Consult relevant research and primary implementations before implementing the fix.
8. Execute independent verification of the correction and affected behavior; record
   the result and limits, then return to the build.

This mirrors the owner's mandatory `diagonosis` skill. FBM can propose new tests,
but cannot edit protected acceptance checks or promote its own patch on its own
score. A faulty test requires separately justified revision, with the old verdict
preserved. Owner or explicitly authorized policy controls promotion. Live Claim
authority, permissions, source truth and final personal judgment are not FBM's
merely because it constructed their implementation. Private runtime records and
shared construction examples retain separate custody and eligibility.

## 4. Evidence status and completion rule

- **Specified:** this register assigns objectives; it supplies no new runtime or weights.
- **Procedure captured:** Alice/FloRA traces record construction methods and results.
  They are not automatically admitted training/evaluation cases.
- **Component evidence:** a documented test or producer artifact applies only to
  its exact source, inputs and claimed behavior. PE/MFM remain in their own lanes.
- **Not established here:** a complete qualified FBM, autonomous backbone repair,
  cross-host builder competence or full consumer assembly.

Track each objective as specified, implemented, component-tested, connected-tested
or behaviorally qualified, with an exact evidence reference and unresolved limits.
Do not promote the entire register from one green test. Before a release claim,
freeze objective-specific thresholds and costs; no arbitrary universal score or
latency cutoff is introduced here. Existing FBM gates remain: **A** eligible case
data, **B** operation transfer, **C** assembled-instance qualification. Continued
maintenance also needs independent repair and recovery evidence under FBM-12–14.
