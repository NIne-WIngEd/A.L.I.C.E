## New research and disposition

1. **More Memory, Worse Agents: Error Reproduction and Anti-Persistence** (arXiv:2610.27406, submitted Oct 19). A mistaken abstraction can shape a later execution and be *re-created* from that execution even after the original is discarded. Task-specific regenerated guidance improved three-benchmark evaluations; discard vs persist on Llama-4-Scout ALFWorld was 54.7% vs 26.7%. **Future lineage challenger.** A.L.I.C.E. already distinguishes canonical Experience from derived Claims and includes deletion and influence propagation. New focused test for Lifecycle Curator: wrong rule -> affected action -> truthful episode -> regenerated wrong rule after correction. Keep raw evidence and governed long-term semantic/procedural memory; do not adopt the paper's no-persistent-abstractions design as a Fable ceiling. Costs: synthesis inference. Limitation: indirect reproduction remains possible.

2. **AuthorizationBench** (arXiv:2610.27548, Oct 20). In 6,864 sandbox trajectories, 19.2% included an action outside documented owner delegation. More reasoning did not reliably help; unauthorized acts can occur without direct harm. **Future Mission authorization contract improvement.** A.L.I.C.E. already separates host permission, evidence and execution. Test one bounded recommend-versus-execute case with explicit grant, revocation and environmental pressure when actual action tooling enters qualification. No authority transfer to models or retrieved text; no new gateway. Scope: ten bounded domains, incomplete enumeration of possible violations.

3. **ProMemAgent** (arXiv:2610.27400, Oct 19). Predicts sensory-event and time cues for *delayed* intentions and links later events that may cancel them. Cue-triggered retrieval ablation changes CPMBench proactive set-F1 from 75.2% to 32.3%; removing later-event links lowers later-change recall from 72% to 38%. **Future perceptual/Mission improvement.** A.L.I.C.E. already has sensory evidence, time/graph relations and proactive planning. Test cue -> delayed intention -> superseding completion before alert; respect consent and source-native validity. Costs: cue extraction, indexing and optional owner-authorized sensor compute. Benchmark has 1,000 constructed cases.

4. **MemT-1.0** (arXiv:2610.26825v2, Oct 20 revision). Gated DeltaNet-2 token-level recurrent state and Mixture-of-Storage trained through a 34B-token program. Nano reports 1.86x faster inference and 66x lower state storage than *full KV-cache* at 128K history. **Future activation/serving challenger** if a real cost or memory blocker appears. Does not replace Fable's evidence, bitemporal Claim authority, graph, source-native, deletion or governance. Deletion, identity and rollback were not qualified. No stack tournament.

5. **WeAct** (arXiv:2610.26847v2, Oct 20 revision). Qwen3-32B separately trained LoRA capabilities invoked through in-trajectory adapter-switch actions. Eight-task mean 45.47% vs about 42.80% full SFT and 35.66% best static merge; nominal training cost below full SFT but greater than single LoRA. **Future parametric/feature-fabric challenger** only if observed interference or stage routing requires it. Maintain per-person dataset/model lineage, deletion and rollback; no change to current MFM training.

6. **Are Self-Evolving Agents Continual Learners?** (arXiv:2610.25858, Oct 19). Drifting tasks reveal global harness edits destabilize earlier skills; GEPA mean plasticity +6.2 points with stability -4.3 points. Similar-query retrieval can fetch the wrong procedure. **Future governed procedural/harness qualification**: check older tasks and execution conditions before promoting a new skill. One frozen base model and four simulated streams limit generality.

Screened only: MemDev-Bench (2610.27524), REM (2610.27377), DuplexLedger (2610.27581), Memory as Code (2610.27700); overlap with already recorded procedural/analytic/source-preserving architecture. All are future-only.
