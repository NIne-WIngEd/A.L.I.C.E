## 1. PreCoMem — predictive, reliability-gated host-belief evolution

**Primary:** [PreCoMem: Predictive Cognitive Memory for Self-Evolving Long-Term Dialogue Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29467v1), arXiv:2610.29467, submitted **October 21**, newly surfaced/recovered October 25; listed accepted NeurIPS 2026. **Classification:** **direct future cognitive improvement** at Lifecycle Curator / host-preference belief projection, not a current authority defect.

**Method.** Preserve an immutable episodic cache plus editable FACT/HYPOTHESIS belief projections with support scores. Retrieve by hybrid lexical/semantic/topic score multiplied by current confidence. Compute semantic deviation + NLI-supported contradiction, discounted by normalized entropy of retrieved candidate scores (a concentration-based retrieval-reliability proxy). Route through **MAINTAIN** (reinforce), **PROFILE** (hold ambiguous cues as revisable hypotheses), or **CORRECT** (softly decay conflicting active facts); a prompted model proposes bounded scalar evidence strengths, deterministic formulas do state updates. Hypotheses may promote, facts persist even when deprioritized. This is NOT authoritative Claim adjudication.

**Evidence / ablations.** On independently released AMemGym (20 synthetic profiles × 11 periods, 943 sessions and 2,200 QA/run; three paired seeds), PreCoMem scored **0.458 overall** versus the reported AWE **0.430**; memory score **0.329 vs 0.296**. Removing PROFILE/CORRECT/MAINTAIN lowers 0.458 to **0.444/0.439/0.418**. Changed-state accuracy **0.704**; stale-state errors **10.8%** vs **19.2% without CORRECT**, and first-period adaptation **76.4%** vs **67.8% without PROFILE**. In DiMoBench constructed gradual drift, accuracy recovered near **84%** vs 78–80% for several comparators; the new test includes keyword/entity decoys. Direct LLM-only memory writing performs worse (2.75/3.15 points on two benchmarks) and higher seed variance. Importantly retrieval-reliability gating delays some true corrections: **14.2% of gold AMemGym transitions**, generally by 1–2 periods. Retrieval confidence is not epistemic authority. LoCoMo controlled latency with gpt-4o-mini: retrieval median/p95 1.95/3.86 s; end-to-end 2.35/4.36 s. Query-token measures exclude separate memory-writing model calls.

**Already covered.** Raw episodes vs derived, host/source-person/self distinction, temporally scoped Claim Authority, provenance/uncertainty, PGMem validity-aware retrieval, MemCalib, JitMem, REALM, and Lifecycle Curator. **Actually new:** experimentally differentiated 3-way uncertain-belief lifecycle with retrieval-quality-conditioned corrections and explicit stable-vs-changing state probe metrics. No new data plane.

**Governance and risks.** Never let a confidence decay supersede authoritative correction or a host's explicit revocation. The paper's one-way HYPOTHESIS→FACT and soft-decay FACT handling are unsuitable as *canonical* bitemporal truth; retain as **derived hypothesis/retrieval-weight** updates only. A source utterance by one person cannot change another host's state. Maintain linked raw source, speaker, belief-version, time, authority, deletion/influence lineage and deterministic rebuild from governed events. A high score concentration can be a convincing but wrong retrieval. Data are mostly benchmarked/simulated, not actual lifelong host consent or correct cross-layer unlearning.

**Action (future only):** At operational host-state learning, test one source/person-scoped longitudinal preference with noise, an explicit high-authority correction, later genuine gradual shift, and final revocation. Compare correct state, provisional hypothesis, retrieval activation and response separately. Verify that no confidence gate delays binding explicit host authority. No implementation now.

## 2. When LLMs Forget What They Believe — recall is not constraint enforcement, and stale reminders can be worse

**Primary:** [When LLMs Forget What They Believe: Characterizing State Integrity Collapse in Long-Horizon Dialogues](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.31314v1), arXiv:2610.31314, submitted **October 22**; EMNLP 2026 claimed by authors. **Classification:** **direct future Cognitive Workspace / permission/mission-state utilization qualification improvement**.

**Method.** StateTrack-Bench separates explicit rule recall from actually obeying it across 5 constraint classes (numeric, format, persona, fact, logical) at 3/5/7/10/12 turns; probes 7 models on 125 scenario-depth combinations each. A structured-state anchoring (SSA) mechanism transcribes active constraints into predicate-like ledger entries, prepends them, and performs bounded compliance repair; authors separately stress test *revoked/updated* ledgers and multiple constraints.

**Evidence / ablations.** Strict joint retention ranges **62.4–100%** across models, with one poor model contributing **47 of 79** failures. **88.6% of observed failures** (84.4% excluding that model) preserve correct rule recall yet violate execution; the main 3–12 turn series has no universal monotone decline. Three-judge cross-check κ=0.89 and 120 human rescoring 93.3% agreement support labels but not live action integrity. The decisive negative **35-case dynamic ledger** ablation: incorrect enforcement of revoked rule **2.9%** from updated dialogue alone, **74.3% when an obsolete 'active' ledger is injected**, **0% when ledger is updated before use** (updated/clean decisions 97.1%). Interaction stress test has 76.5% success on 34 valid two-constraint cases. Anchoring may add **~1.18–1.47× tokens** and **~1.34–2.12× latency** in reported examples.

**Already covered.** Source/host speaker attribution, canonical Claim Authority vs Cognitive Workspace projection, revocation, independent action permissions, Grounding Awareness, SAFE-Mem, AuthorizationBench, Source-Judged Compaction. **Actually new:** executable constraint adherence and *revoked-context stale-reminder amplification* experimentally separated from recall, including an important negative case against naive 'always reinsert memory.'

**Governance and risks.** A derived active-state digest must have authorization, validity and generation/version bindings; rejection/revocation flows must invalidate activation/compacted state before model use. Host directives, source-person statements and A.L.I.C.E.'s proposals cannot be merged. Preserve historical evidence as history, not active control. A language-model self-check is never a permission gateway. The study largely uses one simple constraint and synthetic distractors; it does not prove long-term deployed correctness.

**Action (future only):** At Workspace–Mission–provider execution integration, one three-way test for a live scoped directive: (a) correct recall AND obey active version, (b) explicitly revoke/update and verify stale workspace state cannot reinstate it, (c) do not apply it to another speaker/mission. Measure compliance separately from retrieval; do not bolt SSA onto current MFM.

## 3. HERD — bounded evolving harness skills into weights with two distinct drift guards

**Primary:** [HERD: Harness-Evolving Recursive Distillation for Continual Learning](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.31401v1), arXiv:2610.31401, submitted **October 22**. **Classification:** **future challenger** for governed personal-model/procedural consolidation and future harness evolution, not a current model-construction fix.

**Method.** Evolve external skill rules on verified task outcomes while retaining a bounded set of *high-residual* examples (those rules cannot explain). At each stage distill the context-privileged previous model to its weights using a verified target, and constrain worst-group opening-token KL against **both previous checkpoint** (local forgetting) **and original base** (cumulative general capability drift); frozen teacher/anchor states are reused and dual penalties updated by dual ascent. Only weights deploy; the harness stays inspectable for next training step. This is different from merely keeping a playbook, adapter switching (WeAct), or one-shot context-to-parameters (Cortex).

**Evidence / ablations.** One base model, one 25-stage TRACE+17 stream, three seeds: final mean accuracy **0.706**, acquisition **0.713**, forgetting **0.010**, general ability test **0.774**; Seq-LoRA **0.694 / 0.715 / 0.025 / 0.597**, uniform replay **0.699 / 0.711 / 0.020 / 0.599**. Crucial ablations: remove skills, acquisition drops **4.5** points and average 5.0; uniform residual memory doubles forgetting; remove checkpoint anchor forgetting **0.030**; remove base anchor worst-category general degradation reaches **−0.490** vs **−0.130** full. End-to-end training program reports ~**10,723 seconds** (Seq-LoRA 10,463 sec) across the 25 stages. Model correctness and data are verified benchmark answers, not independent autobiographical/host judgments. Strong results under restrictive **one-task-per-stage, known task boundaries, no revisiting prior tasks** assumption. Rehearsal of prohibited/deleted content not evaluated.

**Already covered.** Experience and outcome gating; separate procedural/harness external memory, parametric personal model, conservative promotion, checkpoint/dataset lineage, rollback, earlier Cortex/WeAct/Subspace Tuning findings, and caution from self-evolving-agent stability studies. **Actually new:** joint residual-example selection and *two time-scale* drift controls on recursive *verified skill→weights* consolidation, with evidence for each piece.

**Governance and risks.** Default 'preserve original model' anchor must not protect corrected/deleted information or impose a frozen personal-identity ceiling. Anchor only on explicitly retained, authorized, stable competencies and maintain replaceable versions/generations. Respect subject/provider rights, evidence provenance, correction and deletion exposure across gradients/checkpoints/rehearsal; promotions must be independently verified and reversible. No direct cross-layer unlearning proof. Multi-stage training costs storage of model generations, reference probe outputs, optimizer/adapter and full update inference; offline-only use.

**Action (future only):** If native personal learning later demonstrates measurable skill interference after legitimate feedback, evaluate a *bounded* same-host before/after task sequence comparing selected rehearsal and two *governed, deletion-safe* anchors, with correct-to-incorrect regression and a revoked-fact negative control. Do not redirect MFM checkpoint-26 sequence.

## 4. CausalCoT — local correction sensitivity from topology-isolated reasoning

**Primary:** [Causal Chain of Thought: Mitigating Attention Leakage via Context Isolation](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.31639), arXiv:2610.31639, submitted **October 22**. **Classification:** **future challenger** for provenance-based causal Mission/analytic Recollection when a real correction-propagation failure appears.

**Method.** Have the model construct a DAG of atomic questions; solve root nodes with source prompt, downstream nodes using **only direct-parent responses**. Edit one premise, recompute descendants rather than allowing unrestricted global prompt to override the edited explanation. This makes influence tests explicit but can omit essential source details.

**Evidence / ablations.** MedQA, GPQA, counterfactual MCQs across Gemma 4B/12B and GPT/Claude models; model-mean targeted perturbation *answer change* improves **+4.8 to +21.2 points** vs best baselines. For GPT-5.4, answer-change rate **26.82% CoT vs 48.43% DAG**; Claude Sonnet 4.6 **9.97 vs 32.04%**. BUT zero-intervention raw task accuracy often **declines**, e.g. GPT-5.4 Mini overall **68.96% CoT vs 66.36% DAG**; graph generation errors and incomplete child context worsen answers. Context-restoration ablation improves accuracy while weakening causal control; better planner-generated DAGs improve smaller-model performance. Requires one graph-generation call plus multiple subquestion calls; five days of GPU work reported for broader experiments. Not a proof of causal truth or formally verified influence.

**Already covered.** Cognitive Multi-Graph, causal/temporal projections, Mission dependency DAG, source native and analytic memory; previous REALM/Tether dependency invalidation. **New:** a *black-box intervention test* of whether an edited premise truly changes downstream outputs, rather than merely appearing as a citation or explanation. It is a reasoning execution strategy, not graph-store replacement.

**Governance and risks.** Generated DAGs are fallible projections. Original evidence, active Claim authority, scope, speaker, uncertainty, deletions and revoked ancestor support remain binding. Derived answers should not become new facts without independent evidence. A more manipulable explanation is not a more truthful one.

**Action (future only):** If a real corrected premise is ignored by multi-step reasoning, test one source-grounded DAG intervention and compare result quality, locality of recomputation and inference cost. No universal DAG requirement or hardware change.
