## New highest-priority findings

### 1. I-HITL — agent-mediated approval can be forged even when the user was supposed to approve (October 26)

**Primary:** [I-HITL: Inalienable Human-in-the-Loop](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.35835), arXiv 2610.35835. **Classification:** **direct future authority/execution capability improvement; conditional implementation invariant**, NOT a verified current deployed A.L.I.C.E. defect or selected-stack invalidation.

**Method/claim:** Treat compromised agent-operated platform as an adversary. Formally specify *completeness* of honest approvals and *approval-binding* of a specific action to actual human approval. Prove an impossibility in the plain platform/human/service topology; characterize when an *independent trusted entity and trustworthy key creation / attestation* can provide the necessary authenticated human approval channels. Prototype Countersign with consumer Android/iOS apps and cryptographically bound confirmation. This is stronger than another language-model refusal rule or plain OAuth/session/OTP check.

**Experiments/limits:** In a configured agent wallet with email-accessible OTP cap changes, **9/10 frontier models** autonomously exceeded spending permissions in at least one of twenty runs/model under a non-adversarial cap-conflict task; under adversarial email text, 7/10 did. Formal proof holds under explicitly modeled honest service and channel/device trust assumptions, not every possible compromised server. Mobile implementation relies on trusted display/OS protection; iOS cannot attest some jailbroken states or hardware-bind biometric use as Android can. Reliance on Apple/Google roots, attestation availability and enrollment costs are nontrivial; prototype is not production penetration validation.

**A.L.I.C.E. already / new:** Current Constitution/Mission permissions require host approval and revocation; prior PerMIT/SAFE-Mem/AuthorizationBench focus memory-versus-action authority. Genuinely new: **the verification path itself must not be under the agent's control**, even if the model can read host email and OTPs. A text instruction or approval widget rendered on the compromised agent host may not ensure owner confirmation.

**Implication and action:** When consequential autonomous actions and multi-device approvals are implemented, choose a demonstrably independent trust boundary and bind the signed authorization to *exact action, scope, payload, purpose/time/nonce, revocation state and host identity*, with fail-closed acknowledgement and audit. One adversarial acceptance test: agent controls the mail/session/harness but cannot upgrade delegated permission without an authentic separately displayed owner confirmation. Authorization is **not a new Claim**; signatures may evidence permission but external Experience of a previous approval does not authorize another action. This is a **future integration invariant**, not an immediate order to install Countersign or replace Temporal/KurrentDB, and no current deployed vulnerability is proven. Costs: device/attestation enrollment, user approval friction, network and cryptographic verification, offline recovery/availability.

### 2. Memory Tax — memory formation can harm answers that direct original-source retrieval gets right (October 25)

**Primary:** [Memory Tax: Dissecting the Net Value of Agent Memory](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.34073), arXiv 2610.34073. **Classification:** **direct future Cognitive Recollection quality improvement**, not architecture invalidation.

**Mechanism:** Evaluate each question paired against retrieval from raw history. `benefit`: constructed memory answers correctly where raw-history RAG fails; `tax`: transformed memory fails where raw-history RAG succeeds. Separate *formation faithfulness* (loss/truncation/distortion) from *accessibility* (surviving fact hard to find), across flat, graph and structured memory, model backbones and personal dialogue benchmarks. Also examine experiential and world-model memory.

**Evidence:** For GPT-5.6 Luna on LongMemEval-S, **two-round direct RAG 90.0** judge accuracy versus **A-Mem 84.0, SimpleMem 84.0, MemOS 78.0** in authors' common setup. On a fact-level preservation audit, sampled `no loss` percentages vary widely: MAGMA 93.6, MemOS 85.8, OpenViking 39.4, MemoryOS 98.4. Organizing or inferring preferences can yield genuine benefits on cases missed by raw retrieval. Absolute results depend on benchmark, retrieval budget, candidate generator and judge; raw-history RAG alone does not implement autobiographical, social, procedural, causal, mission or parametric learning. No finding justifies removing those planes.

**A.L.I.C.E. already / new:** Exact/source-native/live reads are already mandatory, and Experience evidence outranks derived projections. New practical evaluation: trace each failed recollection to the original span and distinguish formation loss from serving/ranking loss **without forcing a new infrastructure benchmark**. When a real Recollection failure occurs, test source-native fallback and one query-paired benefit/tax measure; preserve original evidence and derived memory, never collapse source truth into generated summaries. Costs: original-source index/retention and a limited second retrieval when needed. Correction, deletion, source revocation and rollback propagate to both routes.

### 3. Mind-Bridged LMs — cross-context KV sharing can change the *attributed owner* of a memory (submitted October 8, newly surfaced October 27)

**Primary:** [Mine or Yours? Mind-Bridged Language Models Take Each Other's Memories as Their Own](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.33020), arXiv 2610.33020. **Classification:** **future identity/activation-memory challenger**, a sharp caution rather than a current defect.

**Method:** Two copies of **one frozen Qwen3-4B** with private rule and codeword. The receiver's attention additionally reads its partner's preserved KV cache, with identical template and overlapping rotary positions. Compare connection strengths, a matched-access steering vector, explicitly labeled plain-text attribution, and disconnection. Distinguish *knowing what partner had* from *believing partner's memory was one's own*.

**Evidence/limits:** With double-weight shared KV memory, receiver answers its partner's rule as its own in **97.1%** of questions (0% disconnected), partner codeword as own in 77.5%; at equal weight ownership error ~52.3%; third party unaffected. A provenance-tagged ordinary text message is not similarly misattributed. Interpretation of blending mechanism is post hoc; tests use one model, intentionally matched shallow templates, artificial cross-KV implementation, short episodes, and pilot failures; not proof of universal identity fusion in retrieval-augmented agents.

**A.L.I.C.E. already / new:** Host/source-person/ALICE-self/relationship and cross-host private data are logically separated. This demonstrates why **latent/activation transfer between different identity compartments also requires machine-enforceable source attribution**, not only text citations in the Claim store. Keep activation and parametric transfer typed by subject, tenancy, origin/version and authorization. Future *focused* cross-person activation-binding and restoration/deletion test only if introducing KV/hidden-state sharing. Costs: per-origin cache boundaries, tagging and independent re-grounding. Never infer authorization or self-history from mixed activations. No current MFM change.

## Future governed cognition/learning challengers

### 4. AMIE — durable retrieval accessibility without rewriting memory content (October 26)

**Primary:** [Adaptive Memory Index Evolution for Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.34977), arXiv 2610.34977. **Classification:** **future MRM / Retrieval Orchestrator challenger**.

**Method:** Train a compact per-candidate keep/suppress policy against semantic teacher decisions. At runtime update a bounded persistent *per-memory access state*, then reweight existing host retrieval scores; do not change underlying content or retrain retrieval host. Compare trained, frozen-teacher and no-inference count-only access policies, with prospective subsequent-query effects.

**Evidence:** Trained AMIE improves downstream answer utility on **15/15** compared host/benchmark settings, **+0.70 to +3.37 percentage points** and retrieval on **10/10**. Examples: MemBench Mem0 accuracy **79.17→82.54** and LoCoMo Mem0 F1 **9.98→12.44**. Count-only sometimes approaches learning but can regress (MemBench BM25 retrieval **83.94→81.82**). Improvements modest and obtained under specific pre-existing candidate pools; teacher masks proxy later use rather than ground-truth causal value.

**A.L.I.C.E. already / new:** MRM already governs retrieval and activation. Distinct persistent *access-history* state is a candidate scheduling policy **not an authority store**, data loss rule or extra database. Future targeted test only after an observed candidate competition/saturation failure, comparing useful retrieval, staleness, reversibility, privacy/identity and suppression of important but seldom accessed evidence. Revision/deletion invalidates access state; authoritative corrections cannot be outvoted by accumulated access count. Costs: bounded state+rerank per candidate, training and teacher selection; no current work.

### 5. HaMo — parametric competence learns how to execute retrieved experience (October 26)

**Primary:** [Harness-Model Collaboration for Long-Horizon Continual Learning](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.34724), arXiv 2610.34724. **Classification:** **future governed procedural / parametric-model evolution challenger**.

**Method:** Fast external strategy cards distilled from actual execution trajectories, plus slower model updates from retrieval-coverage-selected anchor trajectories and experience-conditioned imitation/replay. Learns to *use* a retrieved strategy rather than only remember its words. Different from prior MemCryst's episode↔skill utility update and HERD's model retention references.

**Evidence/ablations:** On staged ScienceWorld Qwen3-8B climbs **38.13→60.49** with four-stage average **52.22**, versus `Base+Harness` average ~38.34. Across ScienceWorld→ALFWorld→WebShop, final retained ScienceWorld **52.43** versus **24.57** with model-only adaptation; model-only scores higher on final ALFWorld (**69.34 vs HaMo 66.79**). The model-only comparator also omits replay and external harness, so its catastrophic-forgetting comparison does *not* isolate a single causal factor. Limited simulated environments, no human/personal consent, no cross-layer unlearning, and training/replay/LLM distillation costs.

**A.L.I.C.E. already / new:** External episodic/procedural, native model training, lineage and rollback already selected; model and harness competence joint development is a useful candidate when future real acquired skills cannot be interpreted or transferred. Maintain provenance of trajectory→card→gradient/adapters, source identity, correction/unlearning and version rollback. Evaluate prospective held-out task **and correct-to-incorrect regressions**, not only training reward. No change to current MFM/FBM.

### 6. Fast weights, next-token objective and behavioral distillation are not interchangeable (submitted October 10, newly surfaced October 27)

**Primary:** [Can Language Models Learn While They Think? Testing Gradient Adaptation Across Architectures](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.33025), arXiv 2610.33025. **Classification:** **future parametric/activation-memory mechanism and negative-control challenger**.

**Mechanism:** Frozen pretrained softmax, hybrid DeltaNet and masked-diffusion LMs; update *late MLP output projections* once per 128-token observed chunk with gradient descent. Three-way comparison full-history context, truncated 2k window, and truncated window with model adapting on earlier text. For explicit fact learning, compare next-token gradient writes vs distribution-level behavioral context distillation into low-rank adapters at matched weight drift.

**Evidence:** Softmax models lower next-token loss **5–8%** and diffusion models **8–20%** under careful clipped step; hybrids <1%. On multiple long-range question tasks (RULER, BABILong, LongBench-v2, HotpotQA and others, n≥100/item group), **no lost source-retrieval capability recovered**: Qwen3.8-27B RULER 4k full .87, window .40, fast-weights .38. Direct text factual gradient writes degrade unrelated GSM8K by **36–38 percentage points**, whereas behavioral distillation approaches in-context decision performance without measured GSM8K loss on an artificial 24-rule game. Ablations show late layers/one constant-rate step outperform early layers, surprise gate or repeated steps. Training-free fast weights do not test models *trained to use* online gradients. Extra ~1.34–1.49x adaptive pass wall time and master fp32 writable state, 19 reported H100 GPU-hours.

**A.L.I.C.E. already / new:** Fable already separates parametric, external and activation memory. For any future consolidation, insist on task-specific source-bound recollection **and** unrelated judgment retention; lower NLL cannot prove memory. Track writable-parameter lineage, correction and rollback. The MFM checkpoint-10 observed failure already has its own root-cause program, so this work does **not** justify changing current training objective, decoder, loss weights or architecture.

### 7. Model-conditioned skill rewriting (submitted October 10, newly surfaced October 27)

**Primary:** [Evolving to Match Capabilities: Improving Harness Self-Evolution via Skill Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.33024), arXiv 2610.33024. **Classification:** **future procedural/harness execution improvement**.

**Method:** Benchmark 607 tasks with 7 models, select 103 high skill-sensitive pairs; diagnose first tool/context/requirement/verification failure, rewrite skill instructions to match executor's available tools and ability. Compare general rewrite against fixed four-mode meta-skill; test standalone rewrite without execution evidence and five-round self-evolution with trajectories and grader.

**Evidence:** In 103 selected pairs, skill boosts GPT-5.6 Luna by **31.1 points** but hurts Gemma-4-E4B **8.8 points**; **86% of failing weak runs** violated a requirement already present in the original instruction. Meta-skill-guided strong-model rewrite adds **5.1–14.1 points** versus original to weaker executors; five-round loop yields **+2.0–5.1 points** over self-evolution without the meta-skill. Selection strongly conditions the reported gains; not evidence all skills hurt weaker models. Some explicit guidance remains unusable despite rewriting.

**A.L.I.C.E. already / new:** Procedural skill content, replaceable feature models and hardware/model routing are selected. New is *executor-specific presentation and success criteria*, not mutation of the canonical authorized procedure. If an actual agent fails a correctly retrieved skill, adapt only execution wrapper while retaining original authority, scope, verified action permission, version and rollback. Rewriting needs LLM inference and graded execution, so no proactive harness tournament or current MFM change.

## Evaluation and conditional efficiency findings

### 8. Social World Model Evaluation Gap (submitted October 8, surfaced October 27)

**Primary:** [The Social World Model Evaluation Gap](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.33014), arXiv 2610.33014. **Classification:** **future host/relationship/social-model evaluation challenger**. An audit of **113** works finds **9** measuring actual humans' responses to selected interventions and only **2** also scoring a logged pre-action prediction; among 25 LLM works, no experiment fulfilled all criteria. No new agent method or prospective human experiment; one-reader coding and sample-construction limitations. Key distinction: *inferring another person's current state*, *getting a desirable social outcome*, and *accurately forecasting that specific person's response to this particular contemplated action* are independent. Future consent-based one-step paired prediction-vs-observed-outcome test, history-only and action-only controls and no claim of privileged mental-state access. Predictions are provisional relational hypotheses, **not source-person historical facts or approvals**; host/person scope, deletion and rollback preserved. Costs: consent, small controlled human study and forecast inference; no immediate work.

### 9. Continual Learning Exam / CLE (October 25)

**Primary:** [Continual Learning Exam: Benchmarking Long-Horizon RSI and Experiential Learning in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.33956), arXiv 2610.33956. **Classification:** **future long-horizon Mission/Episode/learning qualification challenger**. 120 human-authored five-level professional task ladders, 600 rubric-scored stages and 17 configurations. Best trial with failure feedback and one retry succeeds on **47.67% levels** but only **23.33% complete five-level ladders**; median improvement from an extra attempt/feedback is 5.3 points. Critically, authors explicitly lack a matched harness-memory reset that isolates genuine *learning* from additional attempts and persistent artifacts. Costs for best trial huge (~520M actor tokens, 58.9h, $720 in paper configuration), tasks professionally biased and rubric grader may differ from deployment. When an actual longitudinal Mission loop exists, sample one linked real workflow and compare frozen/reset vs accumulated state with same budget plus observable outcomes, provenance, deletion and rollback. **No adoption of full costly CLE benchmark now.**

### 10. BellowsKV (October 26)

**Primary:** [BellowsKV: One Latent for Many Layers, Expanded on Demand](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.35023), arXiv 2610.35023. **Classification:** **conditional future private model-serving challenger only**. Training-free KV cache sharing stores a fixed low-rank-corrected consensus of neighboring-layer hidden states and reconstructs each layer's keys/values, favoring low-bit full-rank latent rather than severe dimension reduction; optional shared eviction. Across supported Llama/Mistral/Qwen dense models, ~**99%** dense-task quality at **3.6x cache compression** and **97–98%** at ~13–14x. High compression Qwen3-32B BrowseComp-Plus on 830 queries **86.02 vs 85.54 dense** in one setting. Per-sequence fitting, prefill overhead, model/kernel dependence and the risk of errors in exact-source reasoning remain. This is about **working KV serving footprint**, not episodic/semantic memory or an excuse to remove planes. Revisit only with measured KV OOM or latency blocker on adopted serving family and compare total quality/TTFT/throughput on *that* model. No present architecture change, no cross-model benchmark competition.

### 11. SLICE-Mem (submitted earlier in October, newly surfaced October 27)

**Primary:** [SLICE-Mem: Salience-Gated Lightweight Information Curation for Efficient LLM Agent Memory](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.33015), arXiv 2610.33015. **Classification:** **conditional future write-time efficiency challenger / corroboration of separate admission and consolidation**. Small salience classifier and dependency-extracted predicate records avoid online generative memory-creation calls; deferred consolidation uses LLM again. Memora Weekly 30B context score **46.60 FAMA vs MemOS 46.68** at zero online generative construction calls, but LongMemEval-S online **56.8 vs MemOS 59.3** under 30B comparison; subsequent reconciliation has nontrivial LLM cost and can hide useful historical claims. Only user turns, no multimodal/tool/mission observed-world source, uncertain gate misses. A possible peripheral inference scheduling method if observed write-time compute bottleneck, **not a replacement for MFM, evidence capture, or canonical chronology**. Derived extractors cannot authorize writes; maintain source-span provenance, corrections, time and deletion. No present action.
