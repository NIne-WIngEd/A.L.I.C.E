## 1. LEDGER — individual memory-use credit is often mathematically unidentifiable

**Primary:** Arman Behnam, *Top-k Retrieval Leaves Memory Value Undetermined*, arXiv:2611.03289v1, newly surfaced Nov 3. https://arxiv.org/html/2611.03289

**Classification:** **Future challenger / direct future MRM and outcome-credit validity improvement**; not a current implementation defect.

**Method and evidence:** Models episodes as retrieved memory sets plus observed outcome, with lower-order interactions. A row-space test on the retrieval-incidence design shows whether an individual memory's Shapley value is determined by observed evidence. **Fixed-k retrieval leaves every per-memory value unidentified**, regardless of episode count under the formal model; per-question isolated memories introduce additional nonidentifiability. Across 14 standard benchmarks and 55 tested configurations, **no individual memory** met the identifiability test. LEDGER reports supported values with intervals and labels others **undetermined**. It optionally varies retrieval cardinality/exposure to improve information, and groups always co-retrieved memories. On pooled HotpotQA the identifiable fraction rises ~**0.001→0.797**, with **-2.6 accuracy points** on HotpotQA and **+2.7** on LoCoMo. Novelty relative to MemCryst/DiscernMem is an explicit proof that co-retrieval logs cannot justify independent causal usefulness scores, not another ranking heuristic.

**Coverage/governance/risk/cost:** Existing Experience/Outcome and MRM can represent all of this. Value/usefulness is neither truth nor permission; do not delete a memory because its unidentifiable coefficient is estimated near zero. Any grouping must be derived/rebuildable and retain original evidence/subject/source identities. Exploration adds retrieval/storage/latency and may lower answer quality; model/independence/stationarity assumptions limit the theorem's operational reach. No random exploration in consequential Missions. **Action:** At the eventual point where MRM learns memory-level outcome credit, identify **unknown attribution** rather than invent a score; run a single bounded diagnostic only when such a score will change a real learning/retention decision.

## 2. Heirloom — preserve learned personal adapters across base-model upgrades

**Primary:** Zhuomin Chen et al., *Heirloom: Carrying a Phone’s Personalization Across Base-Model Generations*, arXiv:2611.00650v1, newly surfaced Nov 3. https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00650

**Classification:** **Future challenger** for governed parametric personal-model evolution/transfer, not current MFM formation.

**Method/experiments:** An old on-device base+personal LoRA adapter labels retained **unlabeled inputs** with probability distributions (or public/synthetic substitutes). The old model is unloaded before the new base is loaded; a new adapter learns the teacher's behavior without storing its original private labels. Evaluated Qwen3→Qwen3.5 at 2B/4B, Phi-3.5-mini→Phi-4-mini, and same-architecture refresh over 19 user movie-tagging histories, 100 product-rating users and 10 headline-generation users. Movie-tagging adaptation retained pre-upgrade accuracy across generation changes and stayed within **1.7 percentage points of labeled retraining**; vendor-only adapter lost **15.4/12.1 points** on 2B/4B. A 4B on-phone stored-input migration took **38 minutes, 20% battery**. Removing stored inputs lowers performance, 128-input rating budget constrains fidelity, generation still trails label retraining. Nonpersonal teacher ablation performs substantially worse, supporting personalized-behavior transfer.

**A.L.I.C.E. coverage/new/action:** Fable already requires model lineage, provider independence, personal parametric evolution, rollback. Novelty is an implementation and resource-qualified upgrade bridge when old/new architectures/tokenizers differ and only unlabeled personal inputs remain. A teacher can reproduce old mistaken or revoked preferences; transfer is not proof of correct future judgment, host identity, permission, data retention compliance or full FloRA transfer. Preserve authorized source scope and deletion lineage, teacher/probe hashes, new checkpoint owner consent and rollback. **Action:** Candidate method for later *actual* incompatible-base successor failure; compare held-out relevant and irrelevant decisions, corrected/revoked examples, retained utility and transfer drift. Do not migrate current MFM or impose a phone-sized model limit.

## 3. MemUse — accurate delivery is not actual memory use

**Primary:** Changran Xu et al., *Delivered, Not Used: Evaluating Agent Memory at Its Point of Use*, arXiv:2611.01718v1, newly surfaced Nov 3. https://arxiv.org/html/2611.01718

**Classification:** **Future direct evaluation improvement** for Cognitive Recollection→judgment/MRM.

**Method and evidence:** Records the exact text delivered at each inference step, clusters repeated deliveries, checks prior visible context, follows a bounded credit window of actual tool/actions and later corrections. Agentic evaluator separately judges **relevance, corroboration/contradiction, novelty, behavioral influence, effect**. Programmatic evidence-read checks and nullable judgments reduce hallucinated certainty. **8,000 SWE-bench Verified episodes** from three memory systems have end-to-end solve rate within **0.4 percentage points** of no-memory despite different memory-use profiles; **23.2%** of episodes with beneficial use still fail and **5/16** with harmful use still succeed. Useful memory cannot be inferred from task success alone. This is stronger point-of-use instrumentation than recall-vs-answer benchmark scores.

**Coverage/governance/risk/cost:** A.L.I.C.E. already separates retrieved evidence, original event, accepted Claim and behavioral outcome. The new delta is a bounded observational method for investigating an actual failure where retrieved information did not causally affect a decision. Evidence of usage is **not** proof of causality; an LLM judge and coding environment limit confidence. Logging full personal context carries privacy/storage cost: redact and retain under owner policy, preserve source and identity scope. **Action:** Use on a small failing Recollection-to-Mission/FloRA trace when operational, not a blanket 8,000-episode campaign.

## 4. MemHarmBench — memory injection harms a later session without visible task failure

**Primary:** Zhihua Wang et al., *MemHarmBench: Evaluating Cross-Session Prompt Injection in Memory-Persistent AI Agents*, arXiv:2611.01700v1, newly surfaced Nov 3. https://arxiv.org/html/2611.01700

**Classification:** **Future direct privacy/ingress/Mission qualification improvement**; complements earlier combined change/poisoning and downstream egress findings.

**Method/evidence:** Attacker inserts instructions in earlier messages/web pages/multimodal material. Original injection content is absent from a later *legitimate* triggering session; only its retained memory remains. Rates separately measure memory retention (PMIR), attacker-selected downstream action (ASR), legitimate task deviation (UTDR), and trajectory anomalies (ATAR), mapped to users, workspace owners and external parties. Four evaluated agent/backbone configurations yield **59.38–76.39% conditional target adoption** for indirect injections, **75–90%** for direct injections. On eligible indirect episodes, **55.71%** simultaneously show adoption, formal task completion and no anomaly. In a bounded visual experiment, attacker-target adoption increases from **2.5%** clean to **40%** with injected imagery. All scenarios are synthetic and these are target-adoption proxies, **not proven real-world harms**.

**Coverage/governance/risk/cost:** Existing evidence/Claim separation, untrusted source provenance, owner authority, deletion/revocation and privacy egress already cover the needed planes. Novel delta: test whether content that once flowed through memory becomes an operational instruction after the original prompt is no longer visible, including harm to non-host stakeholders. Retained source text must never become authority or policy; separately test explicit host correction vs untrusted content. Evaluation replay of synthetic workflows costs tool/model calls and risks reproducing attack strings in production logs; isolate. **Action:** When persistent agent actions and ingestion are integrated, one focused *previous-session untrusted source → retained derived memory → later authorized Mission* test; no new service today.

## 5. StateGraph — verify dependency before invalidating derived plans

**Primary:** Yiqi Wang et al., *A State Change Is Local, Its Consequences Are Not: Dependency-Aware State Revision for Long-Term Agent Memory*, arXiv:2611.02912v1, newly surfaced Nov 3. https://arxiv.org/html/2611.02912

**Classification:** **Already covered / corroboration with a future conditional method challenger** to dependency validity (tracked Oct 20), not architecture-invalidating.

**Method/evidence:** Candidate dependency discovery uses entity/task relations, explicit derivations, tool provenance, action preconditions, shared time and causal language. Bidirectional relation typing followed by a **counterfactual check** asks whether the downstream state remains valid without that premise; propagation invalidates a strict unsupported dependency **only when no other support survives**. On **50 hand-authored StateChangeBench cases** (10/15/15/10 at depths 0/1/2/3+), cascade Impact-F1 **79.5%** vs reimplemented CUPMem **64.3%** pooled; at depth 3+, **75.1 vs 49.1**. Verification and alternate-support ablations matter. Complete pipeline approximately **12.5K tokens/case** vs **90.6K CUPMem** under reported setup. **Crucial limitation:** paper explicitly omits action-decision evaluation and premise-gate ablation because the number of runs and some original aggregation records were not preserved; small strata, one model, correlated LLM verification and limited significance.

**Coverage/new/action:** Temporal Claim Authority, Mission preconditions, provenance, source-native evidence, multi-graph dependencies and rollback/rebuild already exist in destination. Useful test: distinguish *semantic relatedness* from *actual unique support* and preserve alternative support when a premise is revoked. Never promote a generated dependency to truth, and never propagate deletion solely on weak similarity. **Action:** Store as future candidate for an *observed* stale-derived-Mission failure only; do not introduce another graph engine, migrate Claim Authority or start cascade benchmark tournaments.

## 6. MEMENTO — teach active context checkpointing as behavior

**Primary:** Yunfan Zhang et al., *MEMENTO: Learning Context Management for Long Context Processing Agents*, arXiv:2611.00244v1, newly surfaced Nov 3. https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00244

**Classification:** **Future challenger** to working/activation-memory scheduling, incremental to CORAL (Oct 31).

**Method/evidence:** Trains Qwen3-8B to decide when and what to write in context-folding notes. Approximately **6K** hindsight-annotated teacher trajectories for SFT, then grouped on-policy RL rewarding parse format, note placement/frequency/novelty, factual answer and optional confidence calibration. Books up to **2.5M tokens** are searched using exact/semantic reads; model active window is 16–20K tokens. On 4,708 book QA cases, trained compaction/folding/backtracking reaches **67.8%**, compared with **36.9%** untrained Qwen3-8B in same folded/backtracking harness and **69.3%** larger GLM-5.3, with different costs. SFT only **59.1%**; SFT+RL without backtracking **66.3%**; ablations identify compaction learning as major source. Training requires trajectories, RL sampling, and judge calls; LLM grading and book tasks do not establish long personal-Mission fidelity.

**Coverage/new/action:** Existing Cognitive Workspace, Temporal durable workflow, source-native reads, Mission progress and canonical Experience distinguish notes from truth. New method trains *when* to preserve reasoning context rather than hard-coded summaries. A checkpoint remains derived/expiring; cannot overwrite Claim authority, erase revocation or promote incorrect summaries. **Action:** Conditional model/workspace-method reference when a real long-running Mission loses crucial evidence through context folding. No immediate harness rewrite or extra memory plane.

## 7. MemSidecar — schedule memory separately from the main action loop

**Primary:** Qijia Zhuang et al., *Who Schedules Agent Memory? A Sidecar Approach to Long-Horizon Tasks*, arXiv:2611.00779v1, newly surfaced Nov 3. https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00779

**Classification:** **Future challenger** for MRM/mission-triggered Recollection and latency.

**Method/evidence:** Global memory and task Workspace memory feed two independently chosen delivery approaches. **Hooks** binds memories to LLM-generated event predicates and proactively delivers them when relevant actions approach. **Foresight** separately predicts upcoming memory needs, asynchronously retrieves and caches candidates, then serves matching and valid entries. On MCP-Persona: **41.23% Hooks, 44.34% Foresight**. Ablating workspace memory reduces scores **4.77/6.22 points** respectively; removing global memory loses **2.56/4.42**. MemoryArena task success approximately **13.41% Hooks, 15.41% Foresight**; retrieval/storage token measurements show costs. Experimental web-task distribution and generated triggers do not establish correct timing for years of personal experience.

**Coverage/new/action:** MRM/Recollection, Mission state, L1/Valkey and Temporal already express trigger/prefetch/caching. The delta concerns **retrieval scheduling** and hiding blocking search latency, not replacing stores. Cached memory must be revalidated for source versions, host/person identity, permission, Claim authority and revocation at **use time**, not just fetch time. Generated predicates may overtrigger and expose sensitive data; foreground authorization still required. **Action:** Conditional latency/coverage improvement if instrumented Mission traces show missed pre-action context or blocking retrieval; no sidecar backend mandate.

## 8. SHIFT — align weight unlearning to a retain-only oracle, not indefinite suppression

**Primary:** Hoang-Chau Luong and Lingwei Chen, *Unlearning by Extrapolating a Shrink-and-Heal Lookahead*, arXiv:2611.00374v1, newly surfaced Nov 3. https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00374

**Classification:** **Future challenger** for governed parametric cross-layer deletion, in addition to MUTE/COP.

**Method/evidence:** Shrink fine-tuned weights toward a saved pre-fine-tuning checkpoint; briefly retrain on the retain set; extrapolate the lookahead's token-level distribution change into subsequent distillation targets. The linearized fixed-point interpretation approximates retraining without forgotten data, rather than minimizing forget likelihood without limit. On TOFU with three Llama bases across 9 deletion settings and 11 baselines, average distance to retrained oracle **0.019** vs next method NPO **0.071** (~3.7× closer); combined mean score **0.633 vs 0.561** NPO. MUSE-News retains more nonforgotten knowledge. Removing retain-set healing or extrapolation degrades ablations; one seed per main setting, training is nontrivial.

**Coverage/governance/risks/action:** Fable already requires source/data/model lineage, real cross-layer deletion, rollback and post-deletion influence testing. Novelty is *target-aligned* deletion versus overshoot; **requires the exact pre-fine-tuning checkpoint and an authorized retain set**, and cannot remove information introduced during general pretraining. Approximate oracle behavior does not certify private-data erasure, safety, no reconstruction or cross-layer echo. **Action:** If/when personal parametric deletion produces actual excess retained influence or utility loss, compare against an appropriately reconstructed retain-only reference on one affected model generation. No immediate model or optimizer modification.
