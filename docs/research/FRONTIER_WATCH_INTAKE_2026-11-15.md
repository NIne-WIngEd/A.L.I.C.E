## 1. Cited but Unused — causal citation provenance is not the same as support

**Primary:** *Cited but Unused: Parametric Provenance in Retrieval-Augmented Generation*, arXiv **2611.17161**, first submitted **November 12, 2026**; https://arxiv.org/html/2611.17161 . **Classification:** **future challenger** to provenance-sensitive Cognitive Recollection/answer validation, not a current authority-contract defect.

**Method/assumptions:** Compare responses with top-5 retrieved passages before and after counterfactual replacement of an agreed factual value. Crucially replacement includes passage **titles**, not only bodies. Persistence of the original answer despite altered retrieved evidence operationally signals parametric contribution even when a citation is factually supporting; shifting to the substituted value signals contextual dependence. Separately compare NLI support verification and ten attribution/contrast techniques. Five Qwen2.5 model sizes (0.5B–14B) plus Llama-3.1-8B; ASQA with 800 questions per condition, deterministic greedy completion and selected decided claims.

**Results/limitations/cost:** Decided-claim apparent parametric provenance ranges **1.2–15.7%** (Qwen 7B **8.2%**, Qwen 14B **13.3%**, Llama 8B **15.7%**) at the default substitution distance. At a closer intervention distance Qwen 7B **4.6%** and 14B **8.5%**; rate varies with intervention size and model family. NLI support scoring is approximately chance for causal origin. Context-free distribution contrasts show some signal but are **not sufficiently accurate for deployment**, especially at low positive base rate. Their earlier bodies-only substitution artifact inflated the measured rate roughly **2x**; corrected evaluation includes titles. Single substitution seed, limited factoid-type coverage and few decided ELI5/QAMPARI examples. Counterfactual queries add multiple generations, token/logit collection and source rewriting; not justified on all live responses. Behavioral substitution is a measurable intervention, not direct proof of internal neurons' origin.

**A.L.I.C.E. already covers:** canonical evidence and bitemporal adjudication, provenance-linked Claims, evidence/parametric/activation memory division and source-native reads. **Genuinely new:** a **causal-attribution test** that differentiates evidence merely corroborating a generated statement from evidence that changed the answer. A citation to supporting text must not be advertised as proof the model relied on it.

**Impact/action:** Future-only, if a real factual correction, temporal-state or source-conflict answer wrongly presents parametric recall as source-grounded current truth. Consider a single controlled counterfactual with source-title and body edits and compare answer changes, while explicitly distinguishing *support* versus *causal influence*. No production citation classifier, replacement model, or current MFM training alteration. Keep Claims/owner permissions, identity, citation lineage, correction/deletion and rebuildability independent of generated citations.

## 2. Actor, Judge, or Teacher? — hindsight action-progress supervision

**Primary:** *Actor, Judge, or Teacher? Understanding Decision Models in Long-Horizon Agents*, arXiv **2611.17167**, **November 12, 2026**; https://arxiv.org/html/2611.17167 . **Classification:** **future challenger** for outcome-driven Mission/procedural learning, not a currently validated MFM correction; distinct from previously tracked Jev-Mem.

**Method/assumptions:** Compare decision models as action **actors** versus complete-trajectory **judges** and action-level RL **teachers** in ALFWorld and WebShop. Separate (a) prefix vs full-trajectory hindsight from (b) predicting eventual success (Value) vs judging whether a step actually advanced task completion (Progress). Inject per-step centered progress scores into GRPO advantages in addition to outcome rewards. Ordered subgoal dependencies distinguish a good predecessor action from an easy premature final action.

**Evidence/ablations/cost:** JEV alone acts at **42.9%** ALFWorld, but training Qwen2.5-1.5B with its hindsight progress judgments raises ALFWorld success **72.8→82.9%** and WebShop **56.8→71.4%**. With hindsight Value instead of Progress ALFWorld falls to **69.3%**, WebShop **68.3%**. In a dependency-controlled intervention JEV's probability assigned to the required prior operation rises **14.6→77.6%** when the tempting easy subgoal is removed. Hindsight/progress is most useful on ordered prerequisites. Authors estimate **$22–$30** teacher-query expense per training run, plus rollouts, GRPO and trajectory custody. Only simulated task environments and short action horizons; outcome and step credit depend on verifiable environment transitions. API decision model can be wrong and cannot certify causal truth merely by rating a trace.

**A.L.I.C.E. already covers:** Experience/Result events, Mission dependencies, procedural failure/repair memory, governed model improvement, and separation of outcome evidence from learned interpretation. **New:** compare hindsight **incremental progress** to outcome-probability judgments when a complete Mission trajectory exists; evaluate a teacher by scoring accuracy, not only by acting accuracy.

**Impact/action:** Future-only. If an observed procedural agent repeatedly ignores prerequisite steps, use one previously completed source-verified Mission and compare action-level progress scores with the actual ordered states and later outcome. Promote no rule without independently evidenced preconditions, verified outcomes, permissions, rollback and held-out regression. No new evaluator infrastructure or current learning-loop change.

## 3. Skill Adapter — host-scoped procedural modification and online DAG routing

**Primary:** *Skill Adapter: Personalized Modification and Online Routing of General-Purpose Skills*, arXiv **2611.17007**, **November 12, 2026**; https://arxiv.org/html/2611.17007 . **Classification:** **future challenger** for procedural/skill state and Mission Graph orchestration.

**Method/assumptions:** Keep general skills separately from learned task/user-specific modification adapters. Train a policy to create local adaptations from instructions and outcomes and a router to preload candidates for future DAG nodes, then re-evaluate applicability at actual execution time using fresh environment feedback. The general task executor is frozen Qwen3-8B; adapters and router are separately RL-optimized.

**Evidence/ablations/cost:** Against SkillRL, reported success **87.14→95.48% ALFWorld**, **72.73→81.53% WebShop**, **21.43→38.57% SkillsBench**. An ablation progression increases mean score from a trajectory-only **29.28** to full system **71.86** through composition, preload, adapter learning and routing. With 1000 distractor skills, complete method's ALFWorld success falls **95.5→80.4%** (baseline 87.2→60.3%), so scaling skill choice is not solved. Multiple RL-trained components, intermediate model calls and maintained skill library incur training/runtime/storage cost. Benchmark 'personalized' task adjustments do not establish enduring person-specific development, reliable delegation or general privacy.

**A.L.I.C.E. already covers:** first-party Mission Graph, procedural skill memory, context-dependent Recollection, owner authority, model/skill lineage and outcome learning. **Genuinely new:** time-of-action re-evaluation of a previously prefetched **host-scoped skill adjustment**; applicability is conditional on current actual Mission node, not only the original retrieval.

**Impact/action:** Future-only when a real repeated Mission applies an outdated or wrong reusable skill. Preserve distinct general and person-specific skill records, evidence-linked applicability, user authorization and explicit revocation/rollback; no fixed skill count or new serving plane. A learned router cannot grant tool permissions.

## 4. ReSHAPE — user-visible alternatives can uncover acceptable plans that requests excluded

**Primary:** *How Should Planning Agents ReSHAPE Their Search Space?*, arXiv **2611.16466**, **November 12, 2026**; https://arxiv.org/html/2611.16466 . **Classification:** **future challenger** for host preference reasoning and Mission planning.

**Method/assumptions:** A user request is an initial constrained search space, not a complete statement of all acceptable trade-offs. Evaluate a revised specification both by its overlap with simulated user's accepted plan set and by whether the same downstream planner finds an acceptable outcome. Compare asking abstract preference questions against showing concrete counterexample plans just outside the originally specified bounds. Fixed hidden acceptance policies in travel/shopping simulations, budgeted feedback, held-out simulator judgments.

**Evidence/ablations/cost:** Across **5,800** agent-revised specifications, a single validated trade-off can raise success of the unchanged planner by up to **4.6x**. Standard agents recovered at most **32%** of acceptable plan mass versus **>52%** for one correct reference trade-off. Showing a concrete plan rather than asking improves search-space F1 **+9.7 points** (95% CI **5.0–15.0**) with Gemini 3.6 Flash and **+11.2 points** with Claude Sonnet 4.6 on travel. Simulated acceptance profiles, fixed/deal-breaker assumptions and narrow domains limit external validity; hypothetical options may be costly or intrusive to generate and evaluate.

**A.L.I.C.E. already covers:** host and preference state, goals, Mission Graph, personal judgment, user authority and grounded alternatives. **New:** plan acceptance is revealed by user reactions to *specific alternatives*; an initial user cutoff should not be unilaterally relaxed based on inferred utility.

**Impact/action:** Conditional future capability improvement if a real Mission planner has no acceptable plan despite near-feasible alternatives. Offer a small concrete trade-off choice to the owner, preserve explicit hard requirements, record consent and scope in Claim Authority, then plan. Never reinterpret a deal-breaker or identity-linked preference silently.

## 5. Goal Encoding in LLMs — causal internal next-goal representations are not durable authority

**Primary:** *Goal Encoding in LLMs*, arXiv **2611.17297**, **November 12, 2026**; https://arxiv.org/html/2611.17297 . **Classification:** **already covered/corroboration** with a **future model-mechanism challenger** for Mission/Cognitive Workspace research.

**Method/results:** In controlled multi-step coding tasks, inspect attention heads representing next subgoal. Intervene on/head-transplant activation to cause the same abstract operation to be realized in a different programming-language environment. Targeted causal interventions suggest the model can repeatedly *re-read* the task and accumulated progress to reconstruct the operative goal, rather than carrying a stable recurrent latent goal across its entire token sequence. Controlled task environment and selective activation intervention cannot establish robust multi-hour goal persistence, identity, permissions or general task planning. Mechanistic probing and transplant require internal model activations, specialist implementation and compute, and are not available to all replaceable APIs.

**A.L.I.C.E. already covers:** explicit durable Mission Graph, owner goals, Cognitive Workspace and activation memory. **New candidate:** carefully re-present explicit authenticated goal + state/progress to native models when answering a concrete observed goal-drift defect. No requirement to trust opaque goal heads as autonomous authority; no current action.

## 6. LoRA effective-rank utilization — conditional personal-adapter retention improvement

**Primary:** *Maximizing Capacity Utilization of LoRA Adapters for Mitigating the Learning-Forgetting Trade-off*, arXiv **2611.17191**, **November 12, 2026**; https://arxiv.org/html/2611.17191 . **Classification:** **future challenger** for governed personal-model evolution, conditional on actual adapter-forgetting evidence.

**Method/evidence:** Standard LoRA can use significantly fewer independent singular directions than its configured rank. Add an SVD-free differentiable Rényi-2 effective-rank objective to the language-modeling loss; compare standard LoRA, norm-bounded alternatives and schedules across ranks, mixed domains, three backbone types and multiple seeds. At `lambda=0.01`, Wiki perplexity is lower at every evaluated rank **>=16** in tested mixed-domain rank sweeps, with near-matched training perplexity in most settings. In a reported rank-128 example, realized effective rank rises from roughly **45.8→125.9**. Retention and training fit can trade off by configuration; extreme regularization/ranks and schedule choice may worsen training. Effective-rank measurement is a proxy rather than proof of personal judgment, provenance or forgetting safety. Extra regularization computes low-rank Gram statistics, adding compute/optimization overhead but no new store.

**A.L.I.C.E. already covers:** PyTorch/Accelerate, governed adapters and parametric-memory evolution, held-out retention/rollback, lineage and unlearning. **New:** diagnosed LoRA capacity utilization as a distinct axis from nominal rank. **Action:** Future-only if a *verified* personal adapter fails useful learning or regresses unrelated abilities **and** measured effective-rank underuse plausibly explains it. One matched adapter/held-out retention check, not new generic rank tournament. **Not a repair for MFM's observed first-choice source-grounding failure**.
