## 1. Semantic Context Handoff (SCH): revision reactivates evidence from completed work

**Primary:** Zheng et al., *Semantic Context Handoff with On-Demand Evidence for Long-Horizon Agents*, arXiv:2611.24357v1, submitted **Nov 18, 2026**. https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24357

**Classification:** **future challenger** for Mission Graph execution, Cognitive Workspace and provenance-linked source-native recollection. Not a present architecture defect.

**Mechanism/assumptions:** Store (1) an editable semantic execution state (constraints, facts versus hypotheses, open/blocked/completed work, phase), (2) append-only actual action–observation evidence indexed by IDs, and (3) a deterministic controller. The model issues a *sparse state delta plus action* in one turn. Controller checks reference IDs and structural transitions, executes the action, archives its actual observation and keeps new evidence visible until acknowledged. Acknowledgement changes exposure, never erases evidence. Routine handoff retrieves active-state support, but **failure/reopening of previously completed work explicitly reactivates that completed item's archived evidence and lineage**; unmatched records can surface through deterministic orphan, recent, and safety-related fallbacks. No independent summarizer/retriever LLM invocation. Semantic state remains a mutable hypothesis, not adjudicated truth. Core delta over older evidence-preserving compaction is lifecycle-triggered reopening of completed-work provenance.

**Controlled evidence:** On 150 SWE-bench Verified tasks with the same controller/state machinery and budget, *active provenance only* averaged **80.00% Pass@1** versus **86.89%** with revision-aware historical recovery, nearly identical total tokens and time (**209.8k vs 208.6k tokens/task; 233.8s vs 237.1s**). Three paired runs favor SCH (exclusive wins/losses **15/4**, **14/4**, **14/4**; paired McNemar p≈0.0192, 0.0309, 0.0309). At complete-system level, the strongest accuracy comparator PACT achieved **83.78%** with **367.9k tokens, 400.2s**, versus SCH **86.89%, 208.6k, 237.1s** (~43% fewer tokens, ~41% less wall-time). The SCH-vs-PACT Pass@1 difference was not statistically significant in one paired comparison, so the significant claim applies to the matched *recovery ablation*, not every baseline. A recent-window control scores ~80.89%, BM25 ~83.33%, active provenance ~80.00% in that controlled setting. Other models/benchmarks show efficiency but less replication.

**Existing coverage and genuine delta:** A.L.I.C.E. already has canonical Experience, Mission Graph/work lifecycle, source evidence, provenance, active workspace, rebuildable projections, Retrieval Orchestrator and Temporal. Novel candidate is the **specific reopen-completed-work -> retrieve completed work's lineage** policy using bounded deterministic handoff. It is not a new evidence authority, graph database or storage tier. Completed/current implementation: no change; later implementation may use the policy when an observed Mission review fails to revisit older supporting evidence.

**Governance, upside, cost, limitations:** Maintain Experience vs XTDB Claim authority; source-person/host/self/relationship separation; owner permissions on every source read; record revised hypotheses and new actions as new evidence; stale/deleted/revoked sources must never reappear through provenance, pending buffers, index or restored context; rollback/rebuild derived state. Extra persistent archive/provenance pointers and selective read-token cost; measured ~208.6k tokens/task, 237.1s/task in test setting, not a guaranteed serving cost. Evidence-ID checks verify addressability, *not semantic entailment*. Coding-only history, manual harness parity and potentially incomplete orphan fallback limit generalization. **Action:** when a real reopened Mission fails for missing earlier proof, test one revision-triggered source re-exposure against active-only context, keeping existing archive and workflow plane; do not launch context/infra tournament.

## 2. Piccolo: data-model co-evolution and loss/correctness divergence in decision training

**Primary:** Ling et al., *Piccolo: Co-Evolving Decision Models and Data through Recursive Self-Improvement*, arXiv:2611.24651v1, submitted **Nov 18, 2026**. https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24651

**Classification:** **future challenger** for FBM's governed model-construction decisions and N0/Judgment capability evaluation. This is not a direct repair for MFM source-grounded native formation.

**Mechanism/assumptions:** Family of 0.8B–27B models emits **candidate-label probability distributions** from decision contexts, including text, images, agent harness and data workflows. Automated research agents propose data and model modifications, run experiments, read structured results, and retain source/recipe/checkpoint/semantic-slice lineage in research memory. Training revises coverage/allocations and recipes; per-example feedback considers both KL/candidate loss and **discrete correct↔incorrect decisions**, plus held-out domain slices. Candidate-labeling/single-token judgment is related to already tracked Jev-style models; novelty for Fable is the *model/data co-evolution evidence and accuracy-regression diagnosis*, not the existence of a new authoritative judgment primitive.

**Experiments/ablations:** On a fixed **341-decision** diagnostic set, target/prediction KL improves **2.19 → 0.66 → 0.64** even though the first stage yields **+78 net correct flips** and subsequent stage **−13**, showing declining loss is not a sufficient success measure. Replacing targeted 10% of an early data recipe scored **56.56** vs matched random replacement **54.45**. Identical recipe on 27B Qwen3.5/3.6/3.8 yielded Vision **79.97/79.59/77.86**, but Harness **80.85/81.36/85.93**: newest backbone is not unconditionally better. Recurrent layer-band reuse on a 27B development model lifted Harness **80.85→87.12** while Text **69.15→69.75**, conditional on task; other repeated layer settings worsened. >1M repeated exposures on a fixed data pool improved Data +0.60 points but harmed Text −1.29 and Harness −1.69. A staged data continuation Qwen3.6 improved Vision **71.89→79.59**, Harness **68.64→81.36**, Data **73.20→81.40**; task/split conditions differ, do not compare as interchangeable. Headline Piccolo-27B Vision leaderboard **75.59** is vendor/benchmark-reported, not proof of personal learning or independent host judgment.

**Existing coverage and genuine delta:** FBM already requires traceable build attempts, feedback, source/data lineage, correction, model generations, tests and host-neutral curricula. N0 has an unqualified semantic/judgment lane, and MFM has native first-copy failure. New implementation insight: **record per-slice paired accuracy flips as well as losses for native-model experiment admission** and consider replacing demonstrated low-value training examples before simply extending the same corpus. Existing fixed training plan and current +16 MFM updates are not superseded by this unrelated decision-task study. Completed/current implementation: no change. Potential later issue-specific use only.

**Governance, cost and limits:** Require rights-aware, owner-neutral FBM train data; private host traces must not migrate into shared builder weights without authorization. Candidate model likelihoods are not bitemporal Claims, owner preferences, permissions, or causal judgment proofs. Preserve signed model/recipe/data lineage, controlled holdouts, deletion/revocation propagation into derived datasets/weights and reversible checkpoint rollback. Huge pretraining/posttraining and multi-experiment model/data budget (up to 27B, hundreds of thousands of samples, broad research sweep); no proof hardware-neutral on-device training; small +2pp targeted-recipe gain and leaderboard self-report constrain attribution. **Action:** in a future FBM or N0 learning trial with real observed decision regression despite lower loss, attach slice-level correct/wrong transition audit to that one experiment. Do not start architecture tournament, new mandatory model family or blind extension of fixed training data.

## 3. Learning with Unlearning (LwU): shared weight capacity for learning and unlearning

**Primary:** Vahedifar, Bozorgtabar and Zhang, *Learning with Unlearning: Synaptic Role Partitioning for Simultaneous Continual Learning and Machine Unlearning*, arXiv:2611.24205v1, submitted **Nov 18, 2026**. https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24205

**Classification:** **future challenger**, narrowly for later parametric personal-model deletion/relearning. **Not** proof of deletion compliance, model influence removal, or an architectural invalidation.

**Mechanism/assumptions:** Compute separate empirical diagonal Fisher weight importance scores on deletion vs retain examples. Threshold/dominance partition into four synaptic zones: preserve important-retain; reset delete-dominant capacity; orthogonalize forget-directed updates against retain-gradient for contested weights; reuse low-importance capacity to learn future tasks. Repair retained behavior using small retained subset with teacher self-distillation, masked over forgotten classes. Does not store exemplars from all previous tasks; does require access to the forget set and sampled retained material. Pre-deletion teacher masking only removes forgotten label outputs, **not all implicit influence in the old teacher's weights**.

**Evidence/ablations:** Tested classification CNN/ResNet-18 on Seq-CIFAR-100, Seq-TinyImageNet and haptic Robotic Tactile Internet (RTI), with class/subclass/instance deletion on CIFAR-10/100 and RTI; 3 task orderings/seeds, evaluation includes per-example U-LiRA membership audit against retraining oracle. On CIFAR-100 instance deletion, one compared method SalUn has forget-set accuracy 74.9% near retrain 69.5%, yet U-LiRA AUC **89.45%**; LwU has forget-set accuracy **95.6%** (far from retrain) but U-LiRA **55.1%**, exposing mismatch of proxies. LwU still fails selective subclass removal: CIFAR-100 CNN **50.0%** and ResNet-18 **74.3%** vs retrain **23.3%/24.0%**; paper explicitly states RTI instance deletion unresolved for all tested methods. Class-incremental learning without replay substantially degrades on long task streams. These are **not LLM experiments** and do not measure textual influence echoes or cross-layer reacquisition. The paper includes component ablations of zoning, projected ascent and teacher repair; empirical gains are context- and target-dependent.

**A.L.I.C.E. coverage/delta:** Existing full cognitive foundation has parametric personal memory, strict deletion/correction lineage, revocation, influence-echo detection, adapter/checkpoint rollback and Experience/Claims authority. Novel candidate is simultaneous per-weight allocation for future learning and selective forgetting when real native personal-model weight influence requires intervention. None of its class-head assumptions may become a Fable memory/model/ontology limit.

**Governance/upside/cost/risks:** Explicitly track deleted and retained source membership, all derived checkpoint generations and any contaminated teacher. Stronger proxy membership metric still does not establish zero influence; tests must include post-deletion prompt/tool/memory reacquisition, model regeneration after revocation and rollback. Requires at least two Fisher/gradient analyses, retained examples, extra distillation, optimizer and per-request model updates plus parameter state; no free instant erasure or on-device budget proven. **Action:** only after a demonstrated Fable parametric deletion gap and governance-approved personal model exists, compare a targeted unlearning repair to permitted clean rebuild and influence-echo tests on that actual scope. Do not change current native MFM training or memory deletion contract.

## 4. Vanguard: vision-grounded verification for speculative agent steps

**Primary:** Mao et al., *Vanguard: Knowing When to Trust the Draft in Speculative Vision-Language Agents*, arXiv:2611.23917v1, submitted **Nov 18, 2026**. https://arxiv.org/html/2611.23917

**Classification:** **future challenger**, selectively for Mission execution with perceptual live UI and observed high per-step target-model cost; no new logical cognitive plane or current dependency.

**Method/assumptions:** For each image-grounded agent step, a small draft model generates four candidate action+reasoning steps. An **independent, vision-conditioned, probabilistic decision model** (Jev-1.13.0 in their implementation) scores whether each candidate truly agrees with current screenshot and goal. Accept the top candidate only if a development-calibrated threshold is reached, otherwise call a larger target model. Candidate actions that parse identically are scored once. Step-level fallback is different from lossless token-level speculative decoding; accepted draft may differ from the target, so guardrails remain mandatory.

**Evidence/ablations:** Mind2Web unseen websites: 2B draft alone **43.8%**, 8B target **63.3%**, Vanguard **66.8%**, with target writing **23%** of steps; task split 64.4% target vs 66.5% Vanguard (26% fallback). Additional cross-family Gemma-3-27B target vs Vanguard: Mind2Web **64.1→66.6%**, AndroidControl **34.1→45.9%**, EB-ALFRED **76.6→78.3%**. Selection-only and fallback-only underperform the complete pipeline; removing screenshot degrades web/mobile judgments; score calibration and threshold transfer matter. Most web/mobile/embodied evaluations are gold-history **step success**, not real transactional long-term closed-loop; smaller BabyAI closed-loop episodes support some accumulation but not consequential user Missions. Approximate speed benefit exists only if `draft-K generation + judge < accepted fraction × large target step cost`; scoring every draft and uploading private UI screenshots to external judge adds cost/privacy risk.

**A.L.I.C.E. coverage/delta:** Existing multi-model, Cognitive Workspace, perceptual memory, source-native/live reads, owner-permissioned Missions and independent action constraints are sufficient. Novel is image-evidence-conditioned confidence-gated small-candidate routing **at each action step**, rather than generic one-shot model selection or self-confidence gating. It does not make Jev a mandatory Fable internal model, nor do scores provide action permissions, postcondition evidence, or source authority.

**Governance/upside/cost/action:** For a later known high-cost live UI workflow only, compare one small draft+judge+target fallback on actual frames (privacy isolated or owner-authorized) with the existing single target, measuring correct completed outcomes, tail latency, dollar/token cost and readback failures. Screenshot evidence and agent action result require lineage; no unauthorized remote perception transfer; no learned threshold may grant tool privileges. Do not modify current N0/MFM model training or start a speculative-serving tournament.
