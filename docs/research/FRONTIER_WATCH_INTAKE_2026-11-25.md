## 1. BiDE-Mem / MemPath — answer requirements rather than merely relevant memories
Primary: Liu et al., From Memory Access to Evidence Sufficiency: Diagnosing and Addressing Demand–Evidence Misalignment, arXiv:2611.32088v1, 24 Nov 2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2611.32088
Classification: future challenger for Recollection / evidence assembly. Complements Nov24 search correction stage diagnosis with requirement-set coverage rather than only delivering a decisive passage.
Method: query-independent proposition and structured assertion/index construction; read-time QueryFrame records entities, answer roles, relations, temporal and state constraints; six lexical/semantic/structured channels fused and deduplicated, reranked to evidence budget; source/speaker/time/dialogue ordering in reader inputs. Formal demand-support mapping has satisfied/partial/conflicting/unmet states, but the measured compact path DOES NOT execute the separate four-state verifier. Do not attribute results to that pilot.
Evidence: LoCoMo 1540 answerable items, shared GPT-5.4-mini reader: 91.49% vs Mnemis 85.26%; multi-hop 87.59 vs strongest baseline 75.89; Top10 90.13. Full vs no explicit demand 78.70, no demand-conditioned retrieval 86.69, standard decomposition 87.60. Annotation coverage associated with accuracy (97.27% complete vs 57.01% incomplete), not causal. LongMemEval 91.80 in reported comparison; shared Qwen reader separate protocol. MemPath subset 250 questions/425 demands not probability sample, 869 valid intervention cases per setting with possibly different membership. Evidence-only recovers 404, combined 476. Two generative operations; measured Qwen path ~2741 input/349 output tokens and batch-amortized reranking 4.35 s/question, not end-to-end latency; preprocessing and additional index overhead. Moderate benchmark evidence; no personal identity/deletion proof.
Already/new: existing authority-aware, multi-plane recollection and source-native reads express this. New candidate demand-specific coverage checks for compound temporal/relational questions. A high relevance score or a complete demand list does not certify truth. Subject and valid/transaction time must remain distinct; only authorized current evidence can be served; derived frames/blocks/cache need lineage, revocation and rebuildability.
Action: on an actual compound recall failure, freeze question and authorized sources, inspect coverage of each required condition, compare assembled evidence to a known sufficient bundle, fix failed stage. No universal extra verifier, fixed demand ontology, index tournament or MFM change.

## 2. Learning What vs Learning How — separate task competence from interface competence
Primary: Lee et al., arXiv:2611.31427v1, dated 23 Nov 2026, newly listed Nov25, https://arxiv.org/html/2611.31427v1
Classification: future challenger for FBM and learned formation interface diagnostics, not a current repair.
Method: task demonstrations across different harnesses plus target-interface rehearsal with task information removed/minimized (e.g. transcribe given answer). Matched 2x2, size/budget controls, four seeds and second model family. SFT 2 epochs, 32k sequences, 8-bit paged AdamW, bf16, H100 80GB; additional RL rollouts with verifier. BFCL native function-call target Qwen3-4B base 44.6%, cross-harness task-only 8.5%, rehearsal-only 32.5%, combined 58.8%; combined 54.9–58.8 across four runs. Interface-only rehearsal can collapse other formats (JSON 33.5 to 0). Combined rehearsal reduces text-formatted calls to ~1% in observed runs. Similar interaction on Reasoning Gym and MedAgentBench, not uniform in all harnesses. Single held-out interface per benchmark, mainly Qwen3-4B; task access deliberately matched, no external-memory variation. Moderate evidence for tested agent training; low transfer evidence to MFM's owned mixed-wire decoder.
Already/new: MFM already separates structural acceptance, native lexical/reference/copy behavior and source-grounding controls. FBM separates interfaces and capability qualification. New candidate task-minimized rehearsal as a discriminator for whether knowledge can be expressed in the target wire format. Current first-token issue is not proven to be interface-only and existing optimizer attribution supports continuation. No output repair or target shortening. Preserve complete source/identity and training ancestry; synthetic interface practice is not autobiographical evidence or independent gold; rollback and deletion eligibility remain mandatory.
Action: only if checkpoint26 evidence contradicts the continuation diagnosis or later specifically localizes interface competence, consider a small matched task/format control before any training change. Do not change current run or weaken consumer.

## 3. Leto — utility-based serving with source-backed memory indexing
Primary: Chen and Paracha, arXiv:2611.33005v1, 24 Nov 2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2611.33005v1
Classification: future challenger for MRM and Recollection; much architecture already covered. Distinct empirical contribution: paired useful/harmful outcomes across personalization, dependent tasks and recall, and explicit distinction of retention versus serving.
Method: atomic propositions point to original adjacent-message spans rather than replacing source; supersession and session summaries; standing profile/task-state rendering plus request-specific retrieval. Initial per-memory utility forecast updated by weighted average of feedback (lexical confirmation/correction or graded outcome); threshold/rank for serving, not deletion. Shared feedback is coarse, not individual causal attribution; forecasts are not trained from feedback in this paper.
Evidence: PrefEval 60.5 vs Hindsight 44.2; PERMA mean completion 76.5 vs 73.4; MemoryArena mean progress 32.01% and chain success 18.33%; LoCoMo 71.82 vs raw RAG 74.61; LongMemEval 73.20 vs RAG 66.40, 1185 vs 9813 retrieved tokens. About 40% of single-hop RAG-correct/Leto-wrong subset has an evidence message with no fact pointer. Evidence recall lexical proxy, generated standing profiles may misstate facts, no isolated component causal ablation shown; paired whole-system useful/harmful rates not per-memory attribution. Storage all histories and write inference, standing context overhead, utility bookkeeping; no end-to-end private cost or deletion evaluation. Moderate benchmark evidence, weak for causal attribution.
Already/new: original evidence custody, source-native access, host state, MRM and outcome learning already planned. Candidate utility serving policy must not hide authoritative corrections, bypass subject/permission scopes, treat approval as truth, or delete a memory because it has low utility. Invalidate projections, utility history and dependent adapters on revocation as applicable; original evidence access remains independently available and policy rollback reproducible.
Action: future only if memory delivery repeatedly harms decisions or omits applicable standing constraints. Compare bounded delivery with/without suspect memory on actual task; keep exact/native fallback for source spans not indexed by propositions. No new store.

## 4. Learning Compact State Abstractions for LLM Agents — inspectable working-state programs
Primary: Chittepu et al., arXiv:2611.31800v1, 24 Nov 2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2611.31800v1
Classification: future challenger for Cognitive Workspace, Mission execution and FBM.
Method: deterministic recursive state-update programs, selected by LLM-guided mutation/crossover and reflector-guided repair against full-history reference trajectories; fitness trades task success against token count. Separate program per predefined family. Only 2/10 initial programs feasible; 16/26 improvements required reflector repair. Held-out environment and token-matched recent-history comparisons. Pick & Place 1.00→0.96 success with 281→82 tokens (71% reduction); Classification 0.50→0.44, 395→169 (57% reduction); Forces actually grows state by 5%, noisy success. BabyAI 95% token reduction with no systematic success difference across three runs. Ten-family search 860 meta-LLM calls, 17.7M tokens and 5948 rollouts; BabyAI additional 8.6M tokens. Low runtime cost once compiled; expensive search and family-specific assumptions, reference model may itself fail, benchmark reward not authority/privacy safety. Moderate evidence for bounded environments, low for general lifelong continuity.
Already/new: existing durable Mission Graph/workspace and source-native recovery. New candidate synthesized, inspectable state-update projections rather than repeated lossy LLM summarization, useful for repetitive workflows. Such programs must be sandboxed, versioned, permission-bound and never own canonical Mission/Claim state. Keep source versions, verification status, identity, correction/deletion invalidation and rollback; discarded working-state fields are not deletion of evidence.
Action: if a repetitive Mission shows measured context or prefill bottleneck, compare one programmatic projection against existing state on real outcomes and revision/reopen case. No genetic-search campaign or permanent state schema ceiling.
