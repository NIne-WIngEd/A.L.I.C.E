## 1. TASK Memory — synchronized authority-gated carriers and use-time failures
Primary: Zhao and Wu, https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34762v1 (25 Nov). Future challenger, high relevance to MRM/activation/parametric recollection.
Method: textual record with scope/version/source; covariance-whitened internal subject key or semantic question-derived key; query gate calibrated with same-relation hard negatives; temporary low-rank edit plus paired adoption/restraint activation vectors. BM25/semantic candidate retrieval then partial forward for gate; hooks unloaded after generation. Multi-hop uses adoption only because restraint may suppress bridge entities. Automatic supersession uses model judge plus address, retiring old text/edit/steering together.
Evidence: preregistered Qwen3-8B 300-world CounterFact composite .811 to .919 with text+steering; zsRE .835 to .892. With address and gated carriers CounterFact library .946 vs BM25 .782; at 3,000 memories address+text+steering .926 vs BM25 .731. MQuAKE 600 questions .690 to .765 with full positive-steering/edit combination. On revision hard test Qwen stale-rate .547 to .100 with automatic supersession and T+S; Llama .480 to zero, but judge misses 14% of true updates on Qwen. Naive coexistence can amplify obsolete values. Single-fact weight patch adds little beyond address; LoCoMo 34.2% vs text-only 32.8% with same 298 tokens; full history 60.3%; literal subject key on LongMemEval fails to improve. Five frozen models; synthetic structured edits dominate. Stronger evidence for controlled conflict than personal reasoning. Cost on one A6000: 4s/edit, 25s/steering pair (86 forward passes), covariance estimate ~700k Wikitext tokens, storage of per-memory vectors/keys/low-rank factors; partial read forward and hooks; no laptop qualification.
Already/new: distinct memory carriers, source authority, temporal validity and revocation already required. New actionable future mechanism: learned use/restraint intervention that distinguishes ignoring valid evidence, transferring it to wrong identity, and stale version interference; simultaneous retirement of carriers. Model judge is not authority: bind active versions to deterministic Claim validity/permission/subject scope, preserve historical as-of reads and conflicting claims. Model-specific derived carrier lineage and cache invalidation, rollback/rebuild, and influence-echo test are mandatory; unloading a patch does not prove unlearning.
Action only on observed correct-evidence-delivered but misused failure: matched evidence with/without reversible scoped steering, unrelated-subject and superseded-version controls; no production hooks or MFM objective changes now.

## 3. MetaDecomp — jointly learn progress distinctions and reusable procedures
Primary: Wang et al., https://arxiv.org/html/2611.33822v1 (25 Nov). Future challenger for Mission Graph and procedural reconsolidation.
Method: outer loop induces abstract task-progress states and transition DAGs from successful executions; when state schema refines, reproject original historical boundaries and reassign supported procedures; inner loop adapts decomposition with live feedback, combines fragments and adds unseen work. Memory fixed at test time. Multiple transitions per state pair.
Evidence: 86.3% mean ALFWorld Qwen3-4B over 3 runs, 134 test tasks; highest mean in 4/5 settings. Single-run ablation full 88.81% ALF / 72.20 ScienceWorld score, fixed schema 79.10/65.02, no historical reprojection 82.84/62.92, no state-procedure links 81.34/66.29. Partial cross-benchmark coverage can hurt versus hidden memory. Training 20.34–65.90M tokens depending setting, inference 42.6–103.3K/task, not always cheaper than initial planner. Code pending acceptance, controlled simulated environments; no proof arbitrary lifelong planning.
Already/new: Mission Graph, procedural memory, FRESH conditions and REALM reconsolidation already cover logical planes. Specific delta is jointly revising progress equivalence classes AND reassigning procedures based on original boundary evidence, not merely rewiring procedure topology. Derived state schemas versioned, reversible, source/subject-bound and cannot overwrite actual outcomes or authorization. Deletion invalidates supported transitions; preserve immutable mission IDs and distinguish hypothesized from verified state.
Action only if actual procedures are reused under wrongly merged progress states: replay relevant boundaries and test one added distinction plus historical reassignment. No fixed DAG-only ontology or Temporal change.

## 4. When Skills Fail to Compose — individual capability is not compatibility
Primary: Lin et al., https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34398v1 (25 Nov). Future challenger for native skill/activation composition, FBM evolution.
Method: independently learned soft prompts on frozen models; known skill order; compare one call concatenated with two explicit intermediate-output calls, neutral insertions, trained partner replacement, repair/rehearsal and internal interventions.
Evidence: mean one-call composition <=7.6% vs 88.2–100% two-call across digit and record-editing models. Partner replacement with individually proficient same-operation blocks reduces median 98.0 to 12.7 and 95.3 to .5 on two Qwen bases (post hoc). At eight composed labels, rehearsal retains standalone median 88.9–99.0 vs .9–22.0 plain repair on smaller bases; extra supervision not equal-budget, transfer to unseen partners remains limited, no universal causal site. Batch1 break-even ~283 requests for one 0.5B repaired pair at equal accuracy, batched ~11,115; repeated per-pair storage/training. Narrow soft-prompt toy tasks, not measured code-skill or Fable operator behavior.
Already/new: independent qualification and rollback already required; new diagnostic is explicit intermediate-output control vs joint execution and same-operation replacement control when individually competent skills fail together. Versioned skill/partner compatibility and preservation of retained behavior must not be inferred from isolated success. No reuse of revoked training or mixing source-person/host/self/relationship adapters. No all-pairs sweep; no inference that current MFM formation failure is a composition problem.

## 6. mu-Bench — speaker ownership, task threading and authorization are separate

Method: closed-loop reactive multi-user speech simulator; tool-free and augmented tracks; two independent semantic reviews with arbitration and deterministic final state/tool checks. Cumulative serialized speech, identity metadata and structured semantic assistance isolate bottlenecks.
