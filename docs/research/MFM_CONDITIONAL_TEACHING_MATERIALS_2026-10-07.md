## Rights boundary

The [CC BY-SA 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en) permits reproduction and adaptation under its conditions, requires attribution for sharing, and applies ShareAlike conditions when adapted material is shared. It does not grant every third-party, privacy, or trademark right. Retain the original licensed raw cache and separately version derived dataset transformations with compatible attribution/license handling; do not relabel public text as Apache-licensed project code.

This source review does **not** decide that every trained tensor is Adapted Material, or that ShareAlike automatically licenses all weights. Record model-training and model-distribution rights separately, with an artifact-specific rationale grounded in the original grant. Existing N0 license allowlists include CC BY-SA families, but that history does not admit E2E or resolve a new artifact's obligations. The implemented conditional preparation retains the inspected original grant, exact terms digest, attribution, review scope, and privacy/split limits. The source grant and owner authorization support this conditional-only training eligibility; a second ceremonial source-selection queue is unnecessary.
