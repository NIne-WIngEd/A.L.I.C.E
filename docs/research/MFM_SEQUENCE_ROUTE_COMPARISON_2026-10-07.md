# MFM conditional sequence and grounded producer routes — 7 October 2026

**Status:** researched construction recommendation, not an architecture adoption,
data admission, training request, or capability result. No model code, weights,
optimizer, private upload, FINAL access, or branch change was made for this note.
Original failed outputs and their lineage remain intact.

**Recommendation:** construct one full formation producer with a fresh semantic
decoder, learned selection from complete caller-owned typed inventories, and
explicitly aligned copying for source literals. Give its fresh sequence weights
real conditional language teaching, beginning with the inspected E2E material
and independently authored full-role caller/world examples. Retain the selected
frozen-source path while preparing these inputs. Do not adopt a compressed Gemma
lexical initializer or a pretrained answer-producing decoder on the evidence
available here. The recommendation is conditional on the actual input, token,
rights and resource census; it does not predict that E2E alone will fix MFM.

The immediate authorized work is material and caller/world construction. A
versioned producer change and its first learning tranche need that construction
to specify what can actually be selected, copied and authored. A codec pass,
better syntax, or lower loss cannot replace positive grounded full-role behavior.

## Governing distinction: licensed V1 versus the selected implementation

[Licensed V1 direction](../MFM_V1_LICENSED_BASE_DIRECTION_2026-09-30.md) permits
a pinned non-IT Gemma derivative and requires separately initialized and trained
formation-specific weights with their own objective and causal contribution.
The base supplies general representation; MFM proposes; the independent Claim
gate owns canonical writes. Fine-tuning Gemma alone and serving its free-form
answers is expressly outside that architecture.

[The selected specialist path](../MFM_V1_SPECIALIST_TRAINING_PATH_2026-09-30.md)
is narrower: source-only frozen representations, no pretrained language-head
answers or training, and independently seeded projection, decoder, embeddings
and output weights. The current [implementation](../../src/cognitive_kernel/formation_v1_specialist.py)
projects 3,840-wide source states to a six-layer, 768-wide decoder and ties its
fresh token embedding to a 262,144-way output head. Lexical initialization from
Gemma would change this selected path even if the broader V1 direction permits
the resulting derivative. Frozen base states computed on generated output
prefixes would also change the source-only feature-bank contract.

The destination remains [full MFM](../MFM_FULL_CAPABILITY_BUILD_PLAN_2026-09-29.md):
memories, episodes, correction and deletion, uncertainty, norms and skills,
missions and outcomes, self and relationships, native modalities and useful
accepted-memory consumer effects. A native structured producer is admissible
only if it preserves all of those meanings and the proposal/Claim boundary.

## Diagnosis: what the completed evidence changes

The mandatory diagnosis sequence was followed for this research: validate the
failure and controls; connect their claim to full MFM; trace source bank, shared
fresh head and task-conditioned decoder; locate correct-prefix departures;
understand the emission and consumer responsibilities; formulate a coherent
construction; consult mechanisms and opposing evidence; state verification and
falsifiers. No corrective implementation is claimed by this note.

[The recovered 576945/576984 record](../MFM_SEQUENCE_FAILURE_DISCRIMINATOR_2026-10-07.md)
reports independently checked pins, complete rosters, objective reconciliation
and later cached/full parity. The 38 formation generations contain zero
positive grounded proposals: 34 malformed, one duplicate-key, one grounding
failure and two valid empty decisions. Source reconstructions also fail parsing.
All 39 non-exact first departures are already incorrect under the correct
teacher prefix. EOS and cached/full agreement therefore do not establish
sequence competence, and an exclusively rollout/cache explanation is rejected
at those departures.

Development value text has 10.79% correct-prefix top1 versus 33.92% on diverse
training cases. Every development first departure requires a source-reference
token never positively taught to formation; rare labels remain poor even on
training. The auxiliary source task supplies only 697 positive token types,
and the combined training targets cover 887. This is not a broad lexical or
conditional language foundation for a fresh 262K head.

Zero states change all 39 first-departure argmaxes; foreign source swaps change
seven formation-training argmaxes. Development paired ranks and probabilities
also change although all nine greedy choices remain wrong. Identical cohort
aggregates under the cyclic foreign permutation do not mean identical per-case
distributions or source blindness. These interventions establish source
sensitivity, not source entailment or encoder adequacy.

This evidence supports a joint lexical/reference coverage and semantic sequence
learning deficit. It does not establish an irreparable fresh decoder, an
inadequate Gemma encoder, pointer sufficiency, or auxiliary interference alone.
The 50/50 task mixture, objective balance, task vectors and optimizer reset
changed together. Repeating that same narrow auxiliary construction cannot be
justified as broad conditional pretraining.

## Mechanisms and opposing primary evidence

**Fresh conditional teaching is possible, but task sharing is not automatic
semantic transfer.** [LiT decoder](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.17376), sections
4–5 and appendix C, trains a Base autoregressive decoder on frozen ViT features
with a 250,000-token SentencePiece vocabulary. Datasets range from 3,000 to
over a million images; training usually lasts 50 epochs with 64-token outputs.
Section 5.2 finds smaller decoders lose performance on textual tasks as task
load grows. Section 5.4 finds no apparent transfer of the targeted skills in
its controls: auxiliary tasks can regularize without teaching the supposed
skill. This counters both “a fresh large head cannot work” and “source
reconstruction will necessarily transfer to formation.” Its paper describes
decoder training from scratch; exact weight initialization was not independently
audited from author code here.

[BART](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.703.pdf), sections 2 and 4–5,
teaches corrupted-input-to-original-text generation; the decoder receives
uncorrupted previous target tokens. Large BART uses 12 encoder and decoder
layers and 500K updates with batches of 8,000 sequences. [MASS](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/song19d/song19d.pdf),
sections 3–4, predicts a masked contiguous span while preventing the decoder
from seeing unrelated target positions. Its multilingual monolingual data are
orders of magnitude larger than our corpus. These support the conditional
objective, not importing their budget, model or trained answers. For our frozen
encoder, compute features from the **corrupted observed input**, not the intact
future target: precomputing intact-document hidden states would leak the task.

**Random decoder does not mean fresh lexical language space.**
[Rothe et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2020.tacl-1.18.pdf), section 3, explicitly
shares the pretrained encoder embedding in BERT2RND; its encoder is fine-tuned.
DiscoFuse contains 4.5M examples, with even the 1% condition using 45K.
[Soltan et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.598.pdf), section 2.1.5,
initializes the new decoder's embeddings from the encoder, freezes those
embeddings with the frozen encoder, and initializes a trainable output head
from them. The sequential stage uses 500K updates with a one-million-token
batch and a compatible 1,024-wide lexical space. [SixT](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2104.08757),
sections 3–4, likewise initializes encoder and decoder embeddings from XLM-R;
its second stage fine-tunes most of the network. It uses eight GPUs with
4,096 tokens per GPU and up to 200K plus 30K updates. These are mechanisms
for inherited language geometry, not positive evidence for our fully fresh head
on narrow examples or a frozen compressed initializer.

**Reduced-width inheritance needs a real construction, not a convenient matrix.**
[Lioutas et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2020.findings-emnlp.250.pdf), sections
3–5, reconstructs pretrained embeddings offline, applies embedding distillation,
and fine-tunes the resulting pretrained sequence model end to end. Its translation
sets contain 167K, 4.5M or 36M pairs, with 32K/37K vocabularies. Table 5 obtains
42.34 BLEU with joint adaptation versus 33.34 when non-embedding weights are
frozen and 20.49 when embedding weights are frozen. This is evidence against
treating a frozen low-rank basis and a new sparse-data decoder as a free lexical
transfer. A future 768-wide lexical derivative would need an explicitly specified
reconstruction/distillation objective and measured conditional behavior; a
random projection, SVD reconstruction error alone, or a lower formation CE
would not establish it.

**Full language reuse has a different boundary.**
[Encoder-agnostic adaptation](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1908.06938),
sections 3–5, injects conditioning into a pretrained GPT-2 decoder's self-attention
and fine-tunes it; this preserves a language generator, rather than importing
encoder features into a fresh generator. [Cold Fusion](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2018/sriram18_interspeech.pdf),
section 3, feeds a fixed pretrained LM's logits through learned projections and
gates while training a sequence model. That exact mechanism uses base-head
information and does not fit the selected source-only no-LM-head path.
[Frozen LLM semantic-vector decoding](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1717.pdf),
section 2, learns a projection into a frozen LLM that reconstructs the target
through its own decoder. It investigates native LLM decoding, not fresh-head
competence; norm interventions cannot be imported as a fix for our architecture.

**Pointers have specific responsibilities.**
[RAT-SQL](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.677.pdf), section 4.4, separates
grammar actions from typed column/table selection. Its [pinned author decoder](https://github.com/microsoft/rat-sql/blob/051e7d35f3092d2c75b64dc0c7f1d791942d4f19/ratsql/models/nl2code/decoder.py)
creates separate pointer memories by type (lines 430–437), scores them through
`compute_pointer` (858–874), and separately mixes copied/generated terminal
values (751–855). This demonstrates an implementable separation between
reference selection and open values, not a proof of complete memory semantics.

The [pinned Fairseq Transformer pointer generator](https://github.com/facebookresearch/fairseq/blob/3d262bb25690e4eb2e7d3c1309b1e9c406ca4b99/examples/pointer_generator/pointer_generator_src/transformer_pg.py#L269-L333)
uses a learned generation probability and sums attention mass into vocabulary
positions using actual source token IDs. It requires exact source/output lexical
correspondence. Scattering every processed source ID would also copy instruction,
context, template and escape tokens; it does not establish original UTF-8 byte
alignment or governed evidence identity.

[Sage et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2020.spnlp-1.6.pdf), sections 3–4, alternates
XML tags and copied document values. Training uses 154,450 documents and outputs
average 32.24 tokens; the task expressly restricts values to verbatim document
content. This is a useful source-literal/structure mechanism and poor evidence
for authored temporal, relationship or uncertainty semantics. [Copying in
low-resource translation](https://aclanthology.org/2024.insights-1.9.pdf) reports
only weak improvements across six language pairs and no enhanced gains in the
low-resource/shared-subword conditions. Copying alone is not a demonstrated
solution to limited data.

[MemReader](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.07877v2), sections 4–5, starts from
pretrained Qwen language competence and uses teacher-supervised memory operations.
[Memory Decoder at Scale](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.27919), appendix F, includes
frozen base-answer probability interpolation. Neither establishes that a fresh
MFM head learns full semantics from this corpus, nor transfers Claim authority.

## Concrete construction specification

### 1. Freeze complete observed caller/world inputs before teaching decisions

Use the [conditional materials audit](MFM_CONDITIONAL_TEACHING_MATERIALS_2026-10-07.md)
to construct independent fictional worlds, opened observations and authorized
read snapshots. Input includes registered entity/scene/mission/workspace
candidates, opened evidence IDs and exact locators, prior claim/episode references,
and explicitly declared uncertainty and disposition-scope candidates with their
descriptions. Include plausible same-type distractors and foreign-scope records.
Labels come afterward. A teacher's chosen ID, latent world truth or future event
cannot be used to build the inventory or source representation.

The existing [catalog codec](../../scripts/mfm/formation_reference_catalog.py)
is transport plumbing. Its catalog digest stays caller-owned metadata outside
the learnable target. New local proposal and uncertainty allocations remain local
bookkeeping, not selections from pre-existing authority. Missing caller candidates
must produce explicit input-contract gaps rather than default IDs or regex
discovery from raw text. Inventory presence does not make a claim true.

### 2. Give the producer three explicit learned emission operations

- **Select a governed reference.** From the current semantic decoder state,
  score the actual caller candidate representations for the declared reference
  type. Supervise the chosen index; use candidate identity/type/scope and semantic
  descriptions, not an arbitrary ID string to regenerate. Resolution checks
  inventory binding, type and scope. Reordering candidates should change the
  selected index but preserve the resolved identity. The selector learns which
  admissible record matters; resolution itself grants no fact or write authority.
- **Copy an observed literal.** Retain a reviewed mapping from eligible opened
  evidence positions to original UTF-8 spans and canonical field encoding. A
  learned source/position or span pointer chooses the literal. Exclude unrelated
  instruction and transport tokens. Repeated identical strings need distinguishable
  occurrences. Span start/end must be valid original byte boundaries, with the
  existing modality/structured locator semantics preserved. This is an explicit
  correspondence, not a new byte-alphabet language model or a raw-input fallback.
- **Author semantic content.** The fresh autoregressive decoder still learns
  value text, relevance, action, subject, epistemic status, time, uncertainty,
  cross-proposal relations and full-role transitions. Copying can preserve a name
  inside authored text; it cannot invent the warranted abstraction or decide
  whether a correction, deletion, episode or relationship change is justified.

A versioned typed action stream or native structured record can serialize these
learned decisions into the existing canonical proposal contract. If selected,
that compiler must be a lossless representation of predicted full semantics;
it cannot repair omitted or invalid model decisions or insert a teacher-selected
fact. Full open values and all role fields remain representable. This note does
not choose native serialization solely to suppress malformed JSON.

### 3. Construct specific conditional teaching tasks for the fresh weights

**First concrete public tier: E2E record-to-text realization.** Pin the inspected
original release and its original [CC BY-SA 4.0 grant](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tuetschek/e2e-dataset/ab0d967dd5729ce455ef14ba9ffafc456a335264/README.md).
The local audit finds 42,061 training rows but 4,862 independent meaning
representations and 40,782 distinct references. Group multiple legitimate
human realizations by semantic MR for split assignment and teaching mass.
Reuse frozen features only for exactly identical complete processed source IDs
and attention masks under the same task, processor, template, and clone bindings.
Slot-order variants can share a semantic MR group but require different feature
entries. If processing maps distinct raw payloads to identical IDs/masks, retain
all original payload hashes and case associations; those raw observations remain
distinct. Assign training mass per semantic MR/reference-set rather than calling
the 42K rows independent histories. Preserve official dev/test ancestry. Use a separate task objective,
never empty formation decisions. Track omitted/added slots: human realization
is not canonical memory truth. Rights admission and artifact-specific attribution
and distribution handling are still required.

**Next conditional language tier: observed corruption to restoration.** For
separately admitted broader public text, generate reproducible span infilling,
sentence order restoration and continuation from only the exposed prefix/context.
Encode the actual corrupted observed input. Distinct task tags state exactly
which output is requested. This supplies broader lexical/syntactic exposure;
do not assume restored text implies formation semantics. No broader ready-to-use
corpus was established by the audit, so a named candidate menu is not yet this
teaching tier. Dolly's passage subset remains held for missing source ancestry.

**Full-role tier: conditional formation from complete worlds and caller snapshots.**
Author diverse meanings and rendering families after input freeze. Cover the
complete current contract, including non-null warranted uncertainty, interval
endings, contradictions, correction/deletion targets, episodes, norms/skills,
missions/outcomes, self/relationship distinctions and native sensory observations.
Include source-withdrawal and permission outcomes and accepted-memory consumer
use. Build semantically contrasting cases where changing subject, evidence,
validity interval or prior claim requires a different decision. Complete
adjudicated absence may supervise negatives; missing fields may not. Replacing
names in the same 13 templates does not add independent semantic families.

Train and measure the three responsibilities separately within the same producer:
reference selection, aligned literal copying and semantic authoring. Record
positive token/type and field/operation coverage; keep role strata visible rather
than hiding them in a pooled CE. Choose task mass from the actual coverage and
learning budget, not another assumed 50/50 mixture or an inverse-frequency recipe
that overfits singleton words. Preserve an exact fresh-seed control and original
failed candidate. No training tranche or mixture is selected by this research.

## Resource feasibility and the lexical-inheritance alternative

The present tied lexical matrix contains `262144 × 768 = 201326592` entries,
about 75% of the approximately 267M parameters. FP32 parameters, gradients and
two Adam moments alone cost about 4.27 GB for 267M parameters, before activations,
temporary logits, source features and runtime overhead. Dense output arithmetic
is roughly `2 × 262144 × 768 = 0.403 GFLOP` per token. These are arithmetic
sizes, not a measured training speed or allocation-fit claim.

If the admitted Gemma lexical matrix uses the assumed 3,840-wide geometry, a
full 262,144-entry matrix has about 1.007B entries, 4.03 GB FP32, and about
2.01 GFLOP per token for dense projection alone. Its exact tensor/config binding
must be checked before an initializer is specified. Expanding the decoder to
match that width is not a small cheap substitution. A 768-wide adapter is
smaller but is a new lexical transfer method whose success is not established
by Rothe, Soltan or SixT.

Frozen source states cost `7680 × source_tokens` bytes in BF16 at width 3,840.
One billion cached source tokens would be 7.68 TB before masks/IDs/metadata.
Therefore use content-addressed shards keyed by exactly identical processed source
IDs/masks and their fixed processor/clone bindings, and measure the new token
census. Semantic MR groups still control split and teaching mass; they do not
define feature reuse. Preserve original payload hashes for every shared entry,
including processing collisions. Do not cache a nominal enormous corpus blindly. Public
source processing can use an authorized public GPU route such as Kaggle without
moving the private 441 bundle. Exact base dispatch, source extraction and
specialist backward capacity still need the actual hardware measurement; “public
GPU is available” does not prove a 12B base fits one GPU. Private processing
retains the protected owner-controlled CPU/no-egress boundary.

If measured public conditional learning cannot meet a feasible construction
budget, revisit B1 explicitly as a versioned lexical derivative: specify which
Gemma tensors are inherited, the full-width or compression mapping, reconstruction
and conditional training objective, lineage, resources and fresh-role causal
contribution. That would be a supported architectural decision to research,
not permission to make the existing random projection look pretrained. B2's
per-prefix base forwards and changed private feature ancestry have an additional
cost and contract burden. Neither is the immediate adopted correction.

## Decisive verification within construction

The next useful learning test is one matched, bounded tranche of the constructed
producer and material, after their actual rights/input/token/capacity checks.
It is not a standing architecture tournament or another qualification campaign.
Retain the original full-role failures and compare source-grounded behavior
against the same seeded producer and declared zero/foreign-source controls.

| Claim | Observable test and falsifier |
| --- | --- |
| References are selected from caller evidence rather than memorized arbitrary names. | Use independently assigned unseen IDs, reordered catalogs and plausible same-type distractors. Resolve selected indices under the externally bound catalog. Wrong resolved identities, target-derived candidates, or dependence on catalog checksums falsify the claim. |
| Copying preserves eligible evidence literally and selects the right occurrence. | Use escaping, UTF-8, repeated literals, foreign records and withdrawn/unopened sources with original locators. Compare selected bytes and locators independently. Successful whole-source reconstruction alone is insufficient. |
| The fresh decoder learned conditional realization. | Hold out semantic input groups and rendering/world ancestry; contrast records differing in a decisive slot/observation. Correct-prefix and free-generation semantic errors remain distinct. If semantic decisions do not respond appropriately to changed evidence, extra text has not repaired conditioning. |
| Full-role formation improved rather than only syntax or literal identity. | Inspect positive grounded proposals and distinct role fields on the original failure conditions plus constructed semantic contrasts; include correction/deletion, uncertainty/time and a real accepted-memory consumer path. Better CE, EOS, catalog resolution or valid serialization without useful meaning falsifies closure of the full-role claim. |
| The specialist is causally necessary and Claim retains authority. | Remove/swap the learned formation component and show loss of useful full-role behavior; exercise unsupported/foreign/deleted proposals through Claim. A base-driven decorative wrapper or unauthorized canonical write rejects the assembled MFM claim. |

A failure here returns to the localized responsibility; it does not automatically
trigger more epochs, a new committee, base-answer fallback or weaker semantics.
Independent FINAL remains sealed and is not part of this construction research.
