The producer's accepted receipt proves local byte custody/counter/idempotency only. A local custody commit with invalid dependencies cannot become accepted replay input. No cross-store atomicity is claimed. Source/identity/commit proofs are never acquired from a future/sibling cut. Current rights are supplied independently from historical presence.

Read notation: **V** is one `read_verification_artifact` surrounded by independent expected VerificationBoundary checks in the R3 core. **T** is one `read_content_part` with the exact new family/entity/version/part and independent expected delivery-boundary checks in W3. **C**, **E**, **S** are the distinct one-part Claim/Episode/source APIs and exact original key shapes in SPEC. **None** means a descriptor/reference has no implicit read. In supplied_bytes_only replay, V is replaced only by the externally bound in-memory original; current service read/permission claims are false, and W3 current assembly still requires the separate current-verification stage. Every original consumed through an actual callback, including privileged proof and cached reuse permissions, is accounted for. No callback conceals a list of reads.
